The feature notes `docs/implementation/minimal-agent-attention-results-2026-10-06.md` (A) and `docs/implementation/prototype-agents-results-2026-10-06.md` (B) hold the exact formulas, the full test inventories, the cost tables and the deviations; the plan is copied next to them. `docs/` is ignored by Git, so the two notes and the plan were added with `git add -f` (this file is already tracked). Feature A, the minimal-agent attention readout. Under `attention_mode="projected_predictive"` the new field `predictive_readout="minimal_agent"` replaces the predictive-kernel content by exact Gaussian conditioning of every receiver on a noisy comparison record. In reference coordinates `xi_i = U_i^{-1} mu_i`, `C_i = U_i^{-1} Sigma_i U_i^{-T}`, head `h` reads the query `a_i = S_h xi_i` with covariance `A_i = S_h C_i S_h^T` and the source utterance `b_j = T_h xi_j` with covariance `B_j = T_h C_j T_h^T + n_h I`, where `S_h` is the existing `raw_score_q[h]`, `T_h` the existing `T[h]` (also the key, so key and content are tied) and `n_h = exp(channel_log_noise[h])` a learned channel noise. With `W_ij = A_i + B_j` the exact one-head posterior is `w_ij = softmax_j(log pi_ij + log N(b_j; a_i, W_ij))` at temperature 1 with no `kl_max` clamp, `r_ij = W_ij^{-1}(b_j - a_i)`, `u_i = sum_j w_ij r_ij`, `Lambda_bar_i = sum_j w_ij W_ij^{-1}`, `R_i = sum_j w_ij r_ij r_ij^T - u_i u_i^T`, and the update `xi_i^+ = xi_i + C_i S_h^T u_i`, `C_i^+ = C_i - C_i S_h^T (Lambda_bar_i - R_i) S_h C_i`, the moments of the exact mixture posterior, so the residual is the derived precision-weighted part and no residual is added. The routing log-prior (causal mask, ALiBi or any registered prior) is the label prior. Under `minimal_agent_inference="exact"` the heads are conditioned in index order by assumed-density filtering, which is exact whenever `S_h C_i S_g^T = 0` for `h != g` and otherwise a declared, order-dependent approximation in which every step is an exact moment match; each pair needs one `d x d` Cholesky factor, computed in one pass over checkpointed pair tiles of about 32 MiB with an online log-sum-exp. Under `"mean_field"` the labels are `beta_ij = softmax_j(log pi_ij - KL(N(a_i, A_i) || N(b_j, B_j)))` at the input moments, and the heads are fused in information form, `P_i^{-1} = C_i^{-1} + sum_h S_h^T Lambda_i^h S_h`, `xi_i^+ = P_i (C_i^{-1} xi_i + sum_h S_h^T eta_i^h)`, `C_i^+ = P_i` with `Lambda_i = sum_j beta_ij B_j^{-1}` and `eta_i = sum_j beta_ij B_j^{-1} b_j`; this covariance omits the between-source spread. The local outputs are `mu_i^+ = U_i xi_i^+` and, under `predictive_covariance="moment_matched"`, `Sigma_i^+ = U_i C_i^+ U_i^T + predictive_covariance_floor (tr C_i / K) U_i U_i^T` (its diagonal for diagonal beliefs, never forming a `K x K` matrix); `"passthrough"` keeps the incoming covariance, and `predictive_covariance_checkpoint=True` recomputes the whole readout in backward with bit-identical values and gradients. The new fields, after `relational_routing` in `vfe3/config.py`, are `predictive_readout: str = "kernel"` (`"kernel"` | `"minimal_agent"`), `minimal_agent_inference: str = "exact"` (`"exact"` | `"mean_field"`) and `minimal_agent_channel_noise: float = 1.0` (initial `n_h`, finite and positive). To enable it, set `attention_mode="projected_predictive"` and `predictive_readout="minimal_agent"`; the config and the operator refuse unequal heads (a mixed `so_n` tower), `content_link_edges` other than `"off"`, and `pos_rotation="rope"` with `rope_on_value=False`, and `inactive_fields()` reports `relational_routing`, `content_link_chart`, `attention_residual`, `relational_output_init`, `kappa_beta` and `projected_factor_route` as unread there. A minimal-agent layer stores `raw_score_q` initialized equal to `T`'s block projector (so the record compares like coordinates at step 0), `T`, and `channel_log_noise` of shape `(H,)`, which joins the relational score optimizer group (`m_relational_score_lr`, weight decay 0); `raw_score_k` and `O` do not exist, so the layer counts `H d K + H` score and `H d K` content parameters. The math is in the new module `vfe3/model/minimal_agent.py`, the wiring in `RelationalOperators.minimal_agent_update` and `relational_predictive_readout`, and every consumer of `raw_score_k`, `O` and the content maps either handles the mode or refuses it with a named error (full-link context, triangle holonomy, routing decomposition, the O-reading operator methods). With a capture the readout records `relational_minimal_agent_gain_fraction`, `relational_minimal_agent_channel_noise` and `relational_minimal_agent_head_coupling` (0 means ADF is exact), which minimal-agent runs write to `metrics.csv` as `val_relational_minimal_agent_*`. In float32 a layer call whose zero-jitter factorizations fail, or, under `projected_cov_condition_recovery`, whose ADF query covariances fail the float32 certificate, is recomputed whole in float64; a float64 failure raises `ValueError`, and no covariance is ever repaired.

Feature B, the prototype-agent block MLP. The new registry mode `block_mlp_mode="prototype_agents"` makes the block MLP the minimal agent model with a static library of `M = block_mlp_expansion * K` template agents `p_a = N(nu_a, diag lambda_a)`, `lambda_a = exp(log_lambda_a)`, with prior weights `pi_a = softmax(log_pi)_a`; the token's working belief `N(xi_i, C_i)` is the receiver, the query and sending maps are the identity and the channel noise is absorbed into `Lambda_a`, so `W_ia = C_i + Lambda_a`. Under `block_mlp_inference="exact"` the block returns the exact mixture moments, `w_ia = softmax_a(log pi_a + log N(nu_a; xi_i, C_i + Lambda_a))`, `xi_i^+ = xi_i + C_i u_i` and `C_i^+ = C_i - C_i (Lambda_bar_i - R_i) C_i`, evaluated in the equal cancellation-free form `C_i Psi_i + C_i R_i C_i`, `Psi_i = sum_a w_ia W_ia^{-1} Lambda_a`. Under `"mean_field"` (the default) the label step `r_ia = softmax_a(log pi_a - KL(N(xi_i, C_i) || p_a))` is exactly `GaussianTemplateBlockMLP.responsibilities`, and the receiver step is the power posterior `P_i = (C_i^{-1} + diag D_i)^{-1}`, `D_i = sum_a r_ia / lambda_a`, `xi_i^+ = P_i (C_i^{-1} xi_i + sum_a r_ia nu_a / lambda_a)`, `C_i^+ = P_i` (no between-template spread). The update is a precision-weighted modern-Hopfield-type retrieval whose values are tied to the template means, and dropout acts on the increment `xi^+ - xi` only. The new fields, after `block_mlp_expansion`, are `block_mlp_inference: str = "mean_field"` (`"mean_field"` | `"exact"`) and `block_mlp_template_init_variance: float = 10.0` (initial `lambda_a`, finite and positive), both read only by `prototype_agents`, and `block_mlp_covariance` gains the value `"posterior"`, admitted only by `prototype_agents` through the registry, which returns `C_i^+` in the input layout (`"passthrough"` returns the input covariance object). To enable it, keep `use_block_mlp=True` and set `block_mlp_mode="prototype_agents"`; optionally set `block_mlp_inference="exact"`, `block_mlp_covariance="posterior"`, and `block_coordinates="reference"`, under which `vfe3/model/block.py::_reference_coordinate_block` passes the whole reference covariance (blocks `(..., H, d, d)` for diagonal beliefs on block frames, else dense) and maps a posterior covariance back as `U_i C_i^+ U_i^T`, so that block is invariant under the passive action. The parameters `nu` (M, K), `log_lambda` (M, K) and `log_pi` (M,), `2 e K^2 + e K` per layer, are initialized as `nu ~ U(-K^{-1/2}, K^{-1/2})`, `log_lambda = log 10`, `log_pi = 0` (an initial pull toward the templates of about 1/11 per layer at unit token variances). All three train at the block-MLP rate; `nu` is decayed by the global `weight_decay`, while `log_lambda` and `log_pi` sit in their own group with `weight_decay=0` (follow-up below). `per_type_hidden_bias` is refused, and `inactive_fields()` reports `block_mlp_activation` and `block_mlp_covariance_floor` under this mode and the two new fields off it. The math is in the new module `vfe3/model/prototype_agents.py`, the class `PrototypeAgentBlockMLP` and the registry entry in `vfe3/model/block_mlp.py`, and the frame helpers `reference_covariance_compact` and `local_covariance` in `vfe3/model/frame_actions.py`; `metrics.covariance_frozen_at_encode` now treats every non-passthrough block-MLP covariance as a rewrite, and the M5 row-11 post-hoc patch refuses this mode.

Deviations from the plan, with the reasons in the notes. Feature A: the mean-field gain fraction is `tr(A M)/d` with `M = (A + Lambda^{-1})^{-1}` at the input moments, not `tr(A Lambda)/d`, which is an unbounded signal-to-noise ratio; a float32 call that fails is recomputed in float64 instead of raising; the exact per-pair cost omits the softmax-invariant row constant `-log det A_i / 2` of `projected_noisy_query_cost`; the mean-field fusion runs as an exact sequential Kalman step instead of a dense `K x K` inverse; the routing is one pass with an online log-sum-exp, the gain fraction and coupling are computed only on request, `projected_factor_route` is also marked inactive, the operator refuses `attention_residual=True` while the config marks it inactive, and `raw_score_k` and `O` are registered as `None`. Feature B: the covariance is evaluated as `C Psi + C R C` and the mean-field `P` as `D^{-1/2} (I + D^{1/2} C D^{1/2})^{-1} D^{1/2} C` (equal in exact arithmetic, without cancellation or `C^{-1}`); the block layout returns the diagonal blocks of `C^+`, the moment-matched block-diagonal member, at `O(N M K d)` instead of forming the dense spread; `inactive_fields()` also reports `block_mlp_covariance_floor`; `matmul_flops_per_layer` is `8 e K^2`, the executed count, not about `10 e K^2`; the two new settings reach the factory through a new `factory_options` registration field; diagnostics are a detached `statistics()` method, not CSV columns. Each feature's review stage corrected one reference-test expectation, with a derivation in its note: the two `factorization_fallback` run-record strings of Feature A, which described the policy before the float64 recomputation, and Feature B's `flops_per_token` expectation under `posterior` on the full layout, which omitted the executed `2 K^3 + 4 K^2` solve.

Follow-up, at the user's three decisions after the integration. First, `exact_record_posterior` skips every pair tile that the routing prior masks in every batch element. Every other output is unchanged, because a masked pair contributes exact zeros to every sum.

## Router clamp reference, diagonal-route GEMM fallback, router diagnostics and RoPE angles (audit 2026-10-17, lane L1)

`routing_kl_max_reference` takes a third value, `"query_shifted"` (audit item 2); the default stays `"absolute"`. Under it `relational_score` asks every routing cost to drop its query-only row constant through a new `drop_query_constant` keyword of the scorers: the Gaussian KL loses -(r + log det G_i)/2 on the diagonal and the dense route alike, noisy_query loses -log det G_q,i / 2, and mean_distance has none. The constant is never formed and subtracted; each route replaces log det G_i by -r (or 0 for noisy_query) in its final sum, so the query log-determinant carries no gradient. `clamped_routing_cost` then clamps the shifted cost from above only, mapping NaN and +inf to `kl_max`, since the shifted cost can be negative and a floor at zero would change beta. Below the cap beta equals the absolute route's exactly, a row shift of the softmax. On the audit's probe (every query variance shrunk by 1e-4, r = 8, kl_max = 4r, causal prior) the absolute reference clamps every scored row and routes by the prior alone, while `query_shifted` clamps none. The posthoc routing decomposition reports itself not applicable under the new reference, since its cap and consistency checks model only the other two.

## Training-loop remediation of the 2026-10-17 audit (lane L3: items 4, 5, 14-17, 21, 34, 35, 47-49, 68, 70)

An exception anywhere in the step loop of `vfe3/train.py` (a training step, an evaluation, a metrics row, a keyboard interrupt) now leaves a record (item 4). `train()` is a thin wrapper around `_train_loop` that attaches a `train.log` file handler in the run directory for the duration of the call and reaps background work before it returns or raises. The loop runs under `try/except BaseException`: on an exception it writes `diagnostics/crash.json` (the failing step, the loop phase, whether the optimizer update had started, epoch and batches consumed, the exception with its notes and traceback, the precision-replay records of the failing step and the last `log_interval` per-step losses), saves the loader's `(tokens, targets)` batch to `diagnostics/crash_batch_step_<S>.pt`, flushes pending metrics rows and replay records, waits for the background best-model save and figure jobs, and writes a resumable checkpoint when the in-memory state is a step boundary. A persistent optimizer step pre-hook tells the two cases apart: before it fires the state is that after S completed steps, saved as `step_<S>.pt` with `batches_consumed - 1` and the RNG streams captured after the batch was drawn; after the step returned (an evaluation failure, a row failure, the consecutive-skip abort) it is that after S + 1 steps, with the CPU stream the evaluation block would have restored. An update that started without the step returning writes no checkpoint and the record says why. The exception then propagates unchanged. The deferred scorer verdicts gained an optional failure-only `detail` in `vfe3/contracts.host_check`: when the verdict raises, the count, finite minimum and first location of the offending entries (`describe_offending_entries`) and the `layer_index` of the registering stack frame are attached as an exception note, with no device work on the success path. `vfe3/relational_scoring.py` passes such a detail at its two raise-only checks (the diagonal route and the no-recovery dense route), a two-line change.

Every `metrics.csv` row carries `row_kind`, one of `log`, `eval` (also when the step is a log step) or `spike` (item 5); the readers are lane L4's. The divergence monitor (item 15) now records every spike event in `divergence_spikes.jsonl` with its severity and a `reported` flag, and rate-limits only the console line and the forced row; an event inside the cooldown whose severity (the largest of x/(10 EMA) over the spiking norms and the loss excess in units of the 1-nat margin) exceeds ten times the last reported severity is reported too, and the EMAs take min(x, 10 EMA) and min(loss, EMA + 1) on spiking channels. On the audit's probe (loss spikes at steps 200, 260 and 330 on a 4.3 baseline) steps 200 and 260 are reported and 330 is recorded. The checkpoint record carries the last reported severity (older records load without it). New warnings (item 14) fire once per layer when `belief_lam_min / belief_lam_max` falls below 1e3 eps32 or the projected-covariance minimum eigenvalue falls below f min_v s_v of the prior table (moment-matched readout with the prior floor only; an alarm in reference coordinates, not an exact bound), and whenever `table_fisher_step_norm_mean` falls more than 100 times within one log interval. ## Revival of `gaussian_frame_diagonal` (audit 2026-10-17 item 3, the user's decision)

The family is back as an opt-in value of `family`, designed for the current architecture rather than restored from `2650b24^`. A belief is $\Sigma_i = U_i\operatorname{diag}(c_i)U_i^\top$, diagonal in reference coordinates, and the model stores the local mean $\mu_i$ as every family does and the reference variances $c_i$ in the `(B, N, K)` sigma slot. The stored $c_i$ is invariant under the passive action $(\mu_i, \Sigma_i, U_i) \to (g_i\mu_i, g_i\Sigma_ig_i^\top, g_iU_i)$, so the family is closed under every structure group, while the old module stored the frame-intrinsic mean in the `mu` slot and needed a coboundary transport seam that no longer exists. Parameter objects of `FrameDiagonalGaussian` (`vfe3/families/frame_gaussian.py`) hold the reference moments $(U_i^{-1}\mu_i, c_i)$, so two beliefs in a shared frame (a token's belief and its prior, or $q_i$ against the transported $\Omega_{ij}q_j$) have the diagonal family's KL, and `frame_diagonal_kl` gives the exact KL across frames through $W = U_p^{-1}U_q$. The class flag `frame_diagonal_covariance` marks the family for every consumer.

The live route reads the reference moments through the realized relational frame. `relational_score` and `RelationalOperators.predictive_covariance_update` move the means to $\xi_i = U_i^{-1}\mu_i$ and evaluate with the identity frame (`frame_actions.identity_frame`), so the comparison covariance is $S\operatorname{diag}(c_i)S^\top$ with no frame product, the diagonal source root is $S\operatorname{diag}(c_i)^{1/2}$, and the readout returns $c_i^+ = \operatorname{diag}C_i^{\mathrm{att}}$ plus the floor in reference coordinates: $fc_{p,i}$ under `prior`, $f\operatorname{tr}(C_i)/K = f\,\mathrm{mean}(c_i)$ under `relative`, their elementwise maximum under `max`, and the Loewner ceiling becomes an elementwise ceiling $c^+ \le c\,c_p$. The mean content is unchanged.

# October 18, 2026

## Training-loop remediation of the 2026-10-17 audit (lane L3: items 4, 5, 14-17, 21, 34, 35, 47-49, 68, 70)

An exception anywhere in the step loop of `vfe3/train.py` (a training step, an evaluation, a metrics row, a keyboard interrupt) now leaves a record (item 4). `train()` is a thin wrapper around `_train_loop` that attaches a `train.log` file handler in the run directory for the duration of the call and reaps background work before it returns or raises. The loop runs under `try/except BaseException`: on an exception it writes `diagnostics/crash.json` (the failing step, the loop phase, whether the optimizer update had started, epoch and batches consumed, the exception with its notes and traceback, the precision-replay records of the failing step and the last `log_interval` per-step losses), saves the loader's `(tokens, targets)` batch to `diagnostics/crash_batch_step_<S>.pt`, flushes pending metrics rows and replay records, waits for the background best-model save and figure jobs, and writes a resumable checkpoint when the in-memory state is a step boundary. A persistent optimizer step pre-hook tells the two cases apart: before it fires the state is that after S completed steps, saved as `step_<S>.pt` with `batches_consumed - 1` and the RNG streams captured after the batch was drawn; after the step returned (an evaluation failure, a row failure, the consecutive-skip abort) it is that after S + 1 steps, with the CPU stream the evaluation block would have restored. An update that started without the step returning writes no checkpoint and the record says why. The exception then propagates unchanged. The deferred scorer verdicts gained an optional failure-only `detail` in `vfe3/contracts.host_check`: when the verdict raises, the count, finite minimum and first location of the offending entries (`describe_offending_entries`) and the `layer_index` of the registering stack frame are attached as an exception note, with no device work on the success path. `vfe3/relational_scoring.py` passes such a detail at its two raise-only checks (the diagonal route and the no-recovery dense route), a two-line change.

Every `metrics.csv` row carries `row_kind`, one of `log`, `eval` (also when the step is a log step) or `spike` (item 5); the readers are lane L4's. The divergence monitor (item 15) now records every spike event in `divergence_spikes.jsonl` with its severity and a `reported` flag, and rate-limits only the console line and the forced row; an event inside the cooldown whose severity (the largest of x/(10 EMA) over the spiking norms and the loss excess in units of the 1-nat margin) exceeds ten times the last reported severity is reported too, and the EMAs take min(x, 10 EMA) and min(loss, EMA + 1) on spiking channels. On the audit's probe (loss spikes at steps 200, 260 and 330 on a 4.3 baseline) steps 200 and 260 are reported and 330 is recorded. The checkpoint record carries the last reported severity (older records load without it). New warnings (item 14) fire once per layer when `belief_lam_min / belief_lam_max` falls below 1e3 eps32 or the projected-covariance minimum eigenvalue falls below f min_v s_v of the prior table (moment-matched readout with the prior floor only; an alarm in reference coordinates, not an exact bound), and whenever `table_fisher_step_norm_mean` falls more than 100 times within one log interval. Every training warning, including the dead-router and covariance-scale ones, is appended to `warnings.jsonl` in the run directory. The dead-router warning (item 34) names every (layer, head) above threshold from per-head tables that `VFEModel._layer_guard_maxima` now leaves in `model.last_router_head_tables`.

Three opt-in training fields were added to `VFE3Config`, `train_vfe3.py` and `tests/profiles.py`, each defaulting to the previous behavior. `max_failed_step_skips` (None) lets up to N steps whose strictest precision rung raised before any update be skipped (gradients zeroed, GradScaler stage reset, no optimizer or scheduler step, the batch saved under `diagnostics/failed_steps/`, counted in the `failed_step_skips` column), and makes a failed evaluation report NaN validation with no best-model update. `max_consecutive_skips` (None) aborts through the crash path after N consecutive rejected updates of any kind. `loss_spike_skip_nats` (None) rejects the update of a step whose loss exceeds the monitor's loss EMA plus the margin, compared on the host loss the step already transfers (no added synchronization), counted in `loss_spike_rejected_updates` (item 17). These counters travel with the checkpoint's divergence record.

`block_variance.csv` (item 35) gained `n_entries` and `n_nonfinite`, extremes taken before the quantile subsample, quantiles of the centered LayerNorm scale s_i = ||Pi mu_i|| / sqrt(K) and of r_i = tr(Pi Sigma_i Pi) / (K s_i^2), computed in float64, and the opt-in `block_variance_at_log` (False) writes the same rows at log steps from a variance-only capture of the diagnostic windows. The per-head split the audit also proposed was not added. The table Fisher step (item 47, `vfe3/table_optim.py`) recomputes rows whose float32 norm overflowed from a float64, max-abs-normalized direction, so a 1e17 gradient no longer freezes its row and a 1e35 gradient no longer writes NaN; rows with a finite norm take the historical float32 step bitwise, and `torch.where(trust > 0, ...)` guards the product. A log-cadence `param_nonfinite_count` column counts non-finite parameter entries. `checkpoint_keep_last` (None keeps all) prunes older `step_<N>.pt` after each successful save, `checkpoint_ms` reports the save time since the previous row, and `checkpoint_at_end` (False) writes a resumable checkpoint after the last step; it is off by default because each checkpoint holds the optimizer moments, several GB at the live scale (item 48). `peak_mem_train_mb` reports the peak of the training phases alone, the diagnostics forward and the evaluation block being bracketed by reads and resets of the peak counter, while `peak_mem_mb` keeps the overall peak (item 49).

The best-model save runs from a host copy of the weights on a worker thread with the atomic replace kept, and the attention-map figure child runs on a background thread, one at a time; `maybe_save_best`, `save_checkpoint`, the finalizers, the crash path and `train()` itself wait for them (item 68). The clip multiply is skipped for a set whose host norm v satisfies v + 1e-6 <= c(1 - 1e-6), where the coefficient is exactly 1, and the gradient finiteness flags use one `torch._foreach_norm` (item 70); `tests/test_train.py::test_grad_clip_per_role_calls_once_per_nonempty_role` now uses a binding threshold, since a non-binding role no longer calls the clip. The frame-class diagnostics (item 21) work per head block in float64 when the basis is supported on the head blocks, read log|det| as traces, and solve eigenvalues once per distinct (phi, Y) row, expanding back by occurrence; values equal the dense computation to rounding.

`tests/test_audit_20261017_l3_training.py` (30 tests) injects the audit's ValueError at a training forward and checks the crash record, the saved batch and that the emergency checkpoint resumes to the uninterrupted losses exactly, does the same for an evaluation failure, and covers the scorer note, row kinds, the 200/260/330 probe, the alarms, the skip and rejection fields, the frame-class equivalence on `block_glk` and `block_glmc` with and without the positional factor, the dead-router naming, the new block-variance columns, the 1e17 and 1e35 table probes, pruning and the end-of-run checkpoint, the peak-memory split, the background save and the clip skip. No GPU test was run.

# October 18, 2026

## Router clamp reference, diagonal-route GEMM fallback, router diagnostics and RoPE angles (audit 2026-10-17, lane L1)

`routing_kl_max_reference` takes a third value, `"query_shifted"` (audit item 2); the default stays `"absolute"`. Under it `relational_score` asks every routing cost to drop its query-only row constant through a new `drop_query_constant` keyword of the scorers: the Gaussian KL loses -(r + log det G_i)/2 on the diagonal and the dense route alike, noisy_query loses -log det G_q,i / 2, and mean_distance has none. The constant is never formed and subtracted; each route replaces log det G_i by -r (or 0 for noisy_query) in its final sum, so the query log-determinant carries no gradient. `clamped_routing_cost` then clamps the shifted cost from above only, mapping NaN and +inf to `kl_max`, since the shifted cost can be negative and a floor at zero would change beta. Below the cap beta equals the absolute route's exactly, a row shift of the softmax. On the audit's probe (every query variance shrunk by 1e-4, r = 8, kl_max = 4r, causal prior) the absolute reference clamps every scored row and routes by the prior alone, while `query_shifted` clamps none. The posthoc routing decomposition reports itself not applicable under the new reference, since its cap and consistency checks model only the other two.

The diagonal comparison route now has the GEMM error estimate and direct-tile fallback that the dense route had (item 11). `_diagonal_gemm_expansion_error` evaluates (1/2) sqrt(r) u max_j rho_j (A + B_j)^2 with rho_j = max_k 1/g_jk in O(N r) per (batch, head) slice, and a slice above `pair_kl_gemm_tol` is rescored by `_diagonal_kl_direct_tile`, which forms a_i - b_j before squaring, and counted in `gemm_fallback_slices`. `pair_kl_gemm_tol` therefore no longer moves a diagonal P(G) onto the dense route; the opt-in adds one host read per layer. On the displaced-first-key probe (r = 32, N = 64, two heads, variances 0.5, float32 inputs against float64 arithmetic on the same inputs) the GEMM's error on pairs below 128 nats was 14 nats at a displacement of 1e3 and 1.6e3 nats at 1e4, and the rescored values agree to about 2e-5 nats in both cases. Three existing tests that pinned the refusal and the dense-route selection were updated.

The projection tile loops of `projected_moments` and `_projected_root_factor` capped a tile at 32 tokens regardless of the budget, so `relational_tile_mib` above about 64 MiB changed nothing there at the live shape (item 6). The cap is now 32 tokens per 32 MiB of budget (`_tile_token_cap`), exactly 32 at and below the default, so the default tiling is unchanged and a raised budget dispatches fewer tiles. The default of `relational_tile_mib` stays 32 pending the CUDA A/B; its config comment now states the cap.

The kernel readout records, at diagnostic cadence under no_grad, the pre-clamp row minimum and maximum of the admissible finite costs (`score_raw_row_min_pre`, `score_raw_row_max_pre`) and, on the diagonal route, the per-head and pooled medians of the four cost terms, query constant, key log-determinant, trace and Mahalanobis (`score_term_medians_pre`, from `diagonal_kl_term_medians` in float64 direct differences). The per-layer diagnostics add `relational_raw_row_{min,gap}_q{10,50,90}`, `relational_capped_mass_{mean,max}` from `(beta * capped).sum(-1)` over scored rows, and `relational_score_term_*_median`; the layer-and-head maxima add `relational_raw_row_min_median_head_max` with its (layer, head) and `relational_capped_mass_mean_head_max`. All have fixed `val_` columns in `vfe3/train.py`. The posthoc KL-cap observer reads the new capture and reports, per (population, layer, head), `raw_row_min_mean`, `raw_row_min_max`, `raw_row_gap_mean` and `capped_beta_mass_max` (item 9).

`build_rope_rotation` forms positions, angles, cosines and sines in float64 and casts the rotation once to the requested dtype (item 44). At N = 4096 and r = 32 the float32 rotation now satisfies max |R_i^T R_{i+5} - R_5| below 5e-7, against 1.7e-4 with float32 angles in the audit. Frame and comparison RoPE change at float32 rounding level; the builders still cache.

`tests/test_audit_20261017_l1_routing.py` covers the five items with tiny models and scorer probes.

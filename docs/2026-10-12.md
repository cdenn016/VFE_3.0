## Deep audit

A deep audit of the tree at `6b872b4` (PR #92) is recorded in `docs/audits/audit-2026-10-12.md`. Six investigators covered configuration, theory conformance, geometry, the model hot path, training and infrastructure, and the analysis layers and test suite, against the user's `GLK_attention_v2.tex`. No source file was changed.

The audit verified causality, gauge covariance under orthogonal reference changes, and manuscript conformance of the routing cost, Gibbs row, moment-matched covariance and decoder. Its most severe findings:
- `block_norm_covariance = "pushforward"` drives the covariance to NaN at the launcher's initialization scale.
- The prototype diagonal mean field has no non-finite flag.
- Frame conditioning has no bound under AdamW.
- The moment-matched floor breaks reference-change invariance.
- The validation target-count check reads a key the writer no longer emits.
- The multi-seed noise floor is published as 2·SD.

On the live launcher 77 of 181 configuration fields are inert, `amp_dtype = "bf16"` among them. The audit lists ten retirement, merge and derivation candidates and six theory-backed proposals. The candidates include the family-chunked decode, latent links and CG coupling. The proposals include a per-layer ELBO diagnostic, a spread-corrected mean-field covariance, a frame spread projection and a prior-anchored floor.

The CPU suite on Linux with torch 2.14.1 ran 4,610 tests: 40 failed, all attributed to missing optional dependencies, figure leaks between tests, or platform-sensitive bitwise and absolute-tolerance comparisons. The GPU lane was not run.

## Audit remediation

The defects of the audit were remediated on `claude/audit-20261012-remediation`, with the user's decisions on four design questions: a trace cap for the LayerNorm pushforward, the frame spread projection as an opt-in field, the prior-anchored floor replacing the old floor outright, and only the C6 and C7 configuration candidates. New tests are in `tests/test_audit_20261012_*.py`.

**Model and theory.** The moment-matched floor of both predictive readouts is now `f * Sigma_p`, the token's local prior covariance (the layer prior, which is the embedding prior under `prior_handoff_sigma = 0`), added in local coordinates by `RelationalOperators.floor_term` (item 4, T5). It involves no frame, so a common reference change by a non-orthogonal `a` (cond 10) now leaves the covariance unchanged to 2.6e-14 or better, where the old `f tr(C)/K U U^T` deviated by 0.1 to 13. It no longer scales with the current covariance: a float32 test whose content maps grow the spread about 5.5 times per layer lost definiteness by the third layer under the new floor, so that test now uses contracting maps, and the guarantee is exact-arithmetic definiteness, not float32 definiteness under geometric growth. The `predictive_covariance_floor` default stays 1e-4. The LayerNorm covariance pushforward is scaled by `min(1, K max gamma^2 / tr)`, the sphere bound on the exact output, in the plain seam and in the `delta_full` composite, and the capped tokens are counted in `layernorm_trace_cap_elements` (item 1). The prototype diagonal mean field flags a non-finite or non-positive `1 + c D` (item 2). The minimal-agent mean-field label step skips the KL symmetry check and the query-factor autograd; values are bitwise unchanged and gradients agree to float32 rounding, because the dropped log-determinant term is multiplied by a row sum that is zero analytically (item 21). The self-edge double count is documented as a trade-off (item 10).

**Frames.** `phi_frame_condition_max` (None by default; added to the `train_vfe3.py` dictionary) bounds `cond_2(U_i)` after each accepted step by limiting the spread of the traceless symmetric part of each head block of every frame factor to `log(kappa_max) / n_factors`, through the logarithmic-norm bound `kappa_2(e^X) <= exp(spread(sym X))`; the window is shifted to keep the trace, so rotation and determinant are unchanged (item 3, T4). It acts on `block_glk` and `block_glmc`, is inert on the compact groups and is refused with the manifold M-step modes. Item 18 was not changed: composed (token, position) frame rows are nearly all distinct, so there is nothing to deduplicate.

**Training and artifacts.** The logged `lr_*` columns show the rate the step used (11); a final validation at the last step is reused instead of evaluated and logged twice (12); position-loss quartiles respect the scored-target mask (13); ablation, scaling and experiment cells restore the process matmul precision, so `fp32_matmul_precision = None` no longer depends on sweep order (14); finalize probe failures reach `summary.json` (15). The baseline launcher refuses a dataset, tokenizer, data seed or token cap that disagrees with the base run (5), and `grad_clip_scope` is a matched baseline field (6b). The refusal of a `minimal_agent` base by the baseline was kept: the readout has `2K^2 + H` attention parameters per layer against `4K^2` (6a).

**Analysis.** The validation scored-target readers accept both writers' keys (7; `finalize_run` does write `final_val_scored_targets`, only the ablation path writes `val_scored_targets`). The multi-seed noise floor is `t_{n-1, 0.975} sqrt(2) s` (8). The figure titles use `metrics.spearman_rho` (16). The readers of never-written keys and the E-step depth figure are removed (17). A contract test runs the real finalize paths and checks the readers' keys (29).

**Configuration.** `kl_max = None` resolves to `8 K` at construction, with the historical 100 pinned for payloads that lack the field (C6). One resolver, `VFE3Config.resolved_hyperparameter`, holds the "None inherits" learning-rate rules (C7); saved fields and `semantic_config_fingerprint` are unchanged, checked against a payload of the pre-change tree. `min_lr_frac > 1` and a negative active `pos_phi_scale` are refused (C12). ## Frame condition bound cost

With `phi_frame_condition_max = 1e4` training fell from about 10 to 5 iterations per second. The bound decomposed every row of `phi_embed` (V x H blocks) in float64 and ran a 64-step bisection over all of them after every accepted step. It now screens each row in float32 with the exact bound `lambda_max - lambda_min <= sqrt(2) ||S_0||_F` of a traceless spectrum, and decomposes only the flagged rows: in float32 with a residual and orthogonality certificate (`tau = 64 d eps_32`), recomputing a row with an uncertified block in float64. Rows passing the screen are untouched. On CPU at V = 50,257, K = 20, H = 2 and `phi_scale = 0.035` with 200 stretched rows, the call took 15.9 ms against 665 ms for the all-row float64 version; the GPU time was not measured. The flagged rows match the float64 projection to 1e-5.

## Audit follow-ups

The user then took up the items the remediation left open. Each was built in its own worktree and merged on `claude/audit-followups-20261012`.

**Configuration retirements.** C1: `decode_mode = "family_chunked"` is removed; saved configs map to `diagonal_chunked` (gaussian_diagonal) or `full_chunked` (gaussian_full), and `laplace_diagonal` is retired. C4: the five `projected_cov_*` and `projected_factor_*` fields are one enum, `projected_factorization` in {`gram`, `gram_certified`, `cholesky_qr2_certified`}, with the tolerances as constants and the recovery precision fixed at fp32; a saved fp64 precision loads and replays with rounding-level differences. The old inactive notices for the factor route under the minimal agent, noisy routing and `rotation_invariant` are gone, so `cholesky_qr2_certified` under those routes runs as `gram_certified` without a notice. C8: `pos_rotation`, `rope_space`, `rope_on_value` and `rope_comparison_covariance` are one enum, `rope_mode` in {`none`, `frame`, `frame_value`, `comparison`, `comparison_invariant`}. C10: `relational_output_init` is fixed at identity and `bch_residual_max` at 1e-6, and `frame_pair_fp64_escalation` with `frame_pair_condition_tol` is one optional `frame_pair_escalation_tol`. C11: `live_config()` and `live_values()` in `tests/profiles.py` build the live launcher route at tiny scale, with `tests/test_live_route_profile.py` (finite forward and backward, causality, one optimizer step). The old test baseline is unchanged. Every migration has a test with an old-style payload. A removed field changes the config hash of every config, so finished ablation and scaling cells are matched through their migrated form; sweeps finished before the change drop out of the cross-sweep cohort table of `summarize_sweeps` (their cells are reused, not rerun).

**Decisions not to change.** C2 and C3 (latent content links, CG coupling) stay as options for the user's experiments. C5 stays: the Euler receiver remains, since it is the exact gradient flow of the profiled energy under state-dependent alpha. C9 stays: both template initializations remain.

**New options, all off by default.**
- T1, `diagnose_agent_elbo`: per-layer receiver-mean ELBO, evidence and mean-field gap in nats for the minimal agent and the prototype agents, logged and written to `diagnostics/`, never in the loss. Checked against Gauss-Hermite quadrature to 1e-10 and direct integration of the declared joint to 1e-8. The gap per receiver was 0.25 to 0.35 nats (one head) and 0.36 to 1.08 (two heads) after one sweep, and 0.12 to 0.32 and 0.05 to 0.62 after eight. With coupled heads the evidence is computed from approximate moments, so the gap can in principle go negative.
- T2, `predictive_covariance = "spread_corrected"`: the mean-field covariance plus the linearized label spread, for the minimal agent with mean-field inference only. On 200 random one-head instances the median KL to the exact posterior fell from 0.180 to 0.074 (99.0 percent of instances improved); it is invariant under a non-orthogonal reference change to 3.7e-14.
- T3, `decode_second_order`: the second-order expected log-loss for the linear decoder with a diagonal covariance, applied in training mode only so validation perplexity stays the plug-in value. It costs about two logits GEMMs per vocabulary chunk. The float64 check against Monte Carlo agrees to about 1e-3 at variance 0.01 to 0.05 and to 0.02 at variance 0.2.
- T6, `relational_routing = "noisy_key"` with `relational_key_noise`: the routing cost KL(q_Q || N(b, V_K + n_h I)) at temperature 1 with a learned per-head noise `routing_key_log_noise`; and the channel-noise M-step `channel_noise_mstep` as a logged diagnostic. Exact prototype inference on the diagonal layout already existed; the polar chart was not built because `phi_frame_condition_max` delivers most of its benefit.
- `relational_receiver_update = "closed_form_profiled"`: the stationary point of the profiled energy for state-dependent alpha, found by a certified scalar root solve per receiver (the closed form is a majorize-minimize step, not the minimizer, because it freezes alpha at its current value). It matches 400 iterated closed-form steps to 1e-12 and has implicit-function gradients checked to 8e-10. Per-coordinate alpha is refused because the coordinates couple through the dense precision. At damping below 1 there is no descent guarantee.

**Host synchronizations (item 22).** A training step makes 25 to 30 host synchronizations, not six; `matrix_exp` alone accounts for 12 to 18. The whole-forward float64 rerun was not built: it saves nothing on the kernel readout, would turn recoverable steps into errors, and CUDA graphs stay out of reach regardless. The prototype-agent and minimal-agent failure checks now go through the deferred host check, and two scalar counts are formed on the device, which removes 5 synchronizations per step on the kernel readout and 6 on the minimal agent. Healthy steps are bitwise unchanged.

**Housekeeping.** `zzzzz.py` is untracked and ignored. The launcher comments for `predictive_covariance`, `relational_routing` and `relational_receiver_update` list the new values.

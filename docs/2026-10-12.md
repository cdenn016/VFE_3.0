## Audit remediation

The defects of the audit were remediated on `claude/audit-20261012-remediation`, with the user's decisions on four design questions: a trace cap for the LayerNorm pushforward, the frame spread projection as an opt-in field, the prior-anchored floor replacing the old floor outright, and only the C6 and C7 configuration candidates. New tests are in `tests/test_audit_20261012_*.py`.

**Model and theory.** The moment-matched floor of both predictive readouts is now `f * Sigma_p`, the token's local prior covariance (the layer prior, which is the embedding prior under `prior_handoff_sigma = 0`), added in local coordinates by `RelationalOperators.floor_term` (item 4, T5). It involves no frame, so a common reference change by a non-orthogonal `a` (cond 10) now leaves the covariance unchanged to 2.6e-14 or better, where the old `f tr(C)/K U U^T` deviated by 0.1 to 13. It no longer scales with the current covariance: a float32 test whose content maps grow the spread about 5.5 times per layer lost definiteness by the third layer under the new floor, so that test now uses contracting maps, and the guarantee is exact-arithmetic definiteness, not float32 definiteness under geometric growth. The `predictive_covariance_floor` default stays 1e-4. The LayerNorm covariance pushforward is scaled by `min(1, K max gamma^2 / tr)`, the sphere bound on the exact output, in the plain seam and in the `delta_full` composite, and the capped tokens are counted in `layernorm_trace_cap_elements` (item 1). The prototype diagonal mean field flags a non-finite or non-positive `1 + c D` (item 2). The minimal-agent mean-field label step skips the KL symmetry check and the query-factor autograd; values are bitwise unchanged and gradients agree to float32 rounding, because the dropped log-determinant term is multiplied by a row sum that is zero analytically (item 21). The self-edge double count is documented as a trade-off (item 10).

**Frames.** `phi_frame_condition_max` (None by default; added to the `train_vfe3.py` dictionary) bounds `cond_2(U_i)` after each accepted step by limiting the spread of the traceless symmetric part of each head block of every frame factor to `log(kappa_max) / n_factors`, through the logarithmic-norm bound `kappa_2(e^X) <= exp(spread(sym X))`; the window is shifted to keep the trace, so rotation and determinant are unchanged (item 3, T4). It acts on `block_glk` and `block_glmc`, is inert on the compact groups and is refused with the manifold M-step modes. Item 18 was not changed: composed (token, position) frame rows are nearly all distinct, so there is nothing to deduplicate.

**Training and artifacts.** The logged `lr_*` columns show the rate the step used (11); a final validation at the last step is reused instead of evaluated and logged twice (12); position-loss quartiles respect the scored-target mask (13); ablation, scaling and experiment cells restore the process matmul precision, so `fp32_matmul_precision = None` no longer depends on sweep order (14); finalize probe failures reach `summary.json` (15). ## Frame condition bound cost

With `phi_frame_condition_max = 1e4` training fell from about 10 to 5 iterations per second. The bound decomposed every row of `phi_embed` (V x H blocks) in float64 and ran a 64-step bisection over all of them after every accepted step. It now screens each row in float32 with the exact bound `lambda_max - lambda_min <= sqrt(2) ||S_0||_F` of a traceless spectrum, and decomposes only the flagged rows: in float32 with a residual and orthogonality certificate (`tau = 64 d eps_32`), recomputing a row with an uncertified block in float64. Rows passing the screen are untouched. On CPU at V = 50,257, K = 20, H = 2 and `phi_scale = 0.035` with 200 stretched rows, the call took 15.9 ms against 665 ms for the all-row float64 version; the GPU time was not measured. The flagged rows match the float64 projection to 1e-5.

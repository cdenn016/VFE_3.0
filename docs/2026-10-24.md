## Exact reference route and the cue/result memory block

The same-state exact route needs no new code: `attention_mode="projected_predictive"` with `predictive_readout="minimal_agent"` and `minimal_agent_inference="exact"`, `norm_type_block="none"`, `block_coordinates="reference"`, `family="gaussian_full"`, and a `prototype_agents` block with `block_mlp_inference="exact"`, `block_mlp_covariance="posterior"` and zero dropout already builds and trains on a tiny CPU model. Under the minimal-agent readout the attention output is the conditioned posterior (`attention_residual` is a kernel-readout knob) and the prototype block returns its posterior mean in place of its input, so no evidence is added twice within a layer.

Added `block_mlp_mode="cue_result_memory"`, the joint cue/result model of the generative-memory plan (Section 3.2): component $a$ has a cue law $\mathcal N(\nu_a, L_a)$, a result law $Y\mid X\sim\mathcal N(\omega_a + F_a(X - \nu_a), Q_a)$ and a prior weight, and the working belief is a Gaussian record of the cue. `vfe3/model/cue_result_memory.py` computes the exact result marginal's moments, the component log weights and the log evidence, with one Cholesky of $L_a + C$ per (token, component), positive-sum covariances, and template chunks bounded by a tile budget merged by the law of total covariance. `CueResultMemoryBlockMLP` in `vfe3/model/block_mlp.py` holds `nu`, `log_lambda`, `log_pi`, `omega`, `log_result_noise` and either a dense `regression_offset` ($F_a = I + D_a$) or `regression_left`/`regression_right` ($F_a = I + U_aV_a^\top$); it starts at $F_a = I$, $\omega = \nu$, $Q = \rho L$, where it equals the same-state posterior mean, and supports the working-variance calibration. Read as a transition, each component is one regime of a switching linear-Gaussian step with Bayesian regime selection, collapsed to one Gaussian.

The mode is restricted to the exact reference route (projected_predictive, reference coordinates, `gaussian_full`, exact inference, posterior covariance, no dropout, no EM term) and rejects anything else. New fields `block_memory_regression_rank` (None or 1 to K) and `block_memory_result_noise_ratio` (0.01) are in `VFE3Config`, the launcher at their defaults, the test profile and the introduction-value table. The log variances and log weights train without weight decay; decay on the regression parameters pulls $F_a$ toward the identity. Parameter-count metadata depends on the rank; the FLOP estimate is the dense-regression bound. This departs from the plan in two ways: the new model is its own block mode rather than a `block_memory_model` selector on a renamed `generative_memory` mode (the rename, Tasks 1 and 2, is not done), and the structured result types and diagnostic schema of Tasks 3 and 7 are not implemented. The reference-diagonal carrier (Task 6) is not implemented either.

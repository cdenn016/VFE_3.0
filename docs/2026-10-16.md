## Selectable moment-matched covariance floor

`predictive_covariance_floor_mode` (in `vfe3/config.py` and the `train_vfe3.py` dictionary, default `"prior"`) selects the floor of both moment-matched readouts. `"prior"` is the prior-anchored floor f Sigma_p adopted on 2026-10-12 (audit item 4, T5): frame-free and exactly invariant under a common reference change. `"relative"` restores the floor that ran before then, f (tr C_i / K) U_i U_i^T with C_i = U_i^{-1} Sigma_i U_i^{-T}, added in local coordinates after the sandwich (equal in exact arithmetic to the old ridge inside C_att). It scales with the input covariance, which is what the earlier runs had, and it changes under a non-orthogonal common reference change, which is why it was replaced. `RelationalOperators.relative_floor_term` implements it for diagonal, full and head-block covariances on block and dense frames; the ceiling still anchors to Sigma_p. The field is marked inert outside the moment-matched readouts, recorded in the run's pure-path record, and pinned to `"prior"` by `fully_gauge_covariant`.

`tests/test_predictive_covariance_floor_mode_20261016.py` checks the relative floor against a dense float64 construction (diagonal and full, batched and unbatched frames), that switching the mode changes the update by exactly the floor difference, that the relative floor scales linearly with the input covariance, that it refuses a missing frame, and the config default, validation, inactive marking and wiring into the model.

## Selectable moment-matched covariance floor

`predictive_covariance_floor_mode` (in `vfe3/config.py` and the `train_vfe3.py` dictionary, default `"prior"`) selects the floor of both moment-matched readouts. `"prior"` is the prior-anchored floor f Sigma_p adopted on 2026-10-12 (audit item 4, T5): frame-free and exactly invariant under a common reference change. `"relative"` restores the floor that ran before then, f (tr C_i / K) U_i U_i^T with C_i = U_i^{-1} Sigma_i U_i^{-T}, added in local coordinates after the sandwich (equal in exact arithmetic to the old ridge inside C_att). It scales with the input covariance, which is what the earlier runs had, and it changes under a non-orthogonal common reference change, which is why it was replaced. `RelationalOperators.relative_floor_term` implements it for diagonal, full and head-block covariances on block and dense frames; the ceiling still anchors to Sigma_p. The field is marked inert outside the moment-matched readouts, recorded in the run's pure-path record, and pinned to `"prior"` by `fully_gauge_covariant`.

`tests/test_predictive_covariance_floor_mode_20261016.py` checks the relative floor against a dense float64 construction (diagonal and full, batched and unbatched frames), that switching the mode changes the update by exactly the floor difference, that the relative floor scales linearly with the input covariance, that it refuses a missing frame, and the config default, validation, inactive marking and wiring into the model.

## Block-local groups keep only their local basis (gauge-group buildout, block_glmc M2)

`block_so`, `block_co` and `block_glmc` already had compact per-head transport (audit 2026-10-10, item 26), but each group still built its dense (H m, K, K) generator basis at construction, about 1 GB for `block_glmc` at K=256, H=8, and `head_block_generators` cached an (H m, H, d, d) copy of it, H times larger than the information it holds. `_build_block_local` now stores the lazy `_BlockLocalBasis(K, n_heads, local, dtype, device)` instead, the (m, d, d) local basis copied into every head, and builds the dense tensor only when `GaugeGroup.generators` is read. `GaugeGroup.certified_block_local_basis()` returns the local basis while the stored basis is still its exact copy (the lazy source, or the tensor materialized from it, unchanged and not trainable); a replaced or mutated basis withdraws it, as for the elementary `block_glk` basis, and `apply_generators` keeps it across a device or dtype move. The readers that previously forced the dense basis now read the local one. The compact transport embeds a head as `M_h = sum_a phi_(h,a) L_a` (an H-fold smaller contraction than the head-block einsum), `embed_algebra` builds its block-diagonal output from the local basis on the no-grad diagnostic path, `head_block_generators` and `generator_traces` are assembled from it, and the new `GaugeGroup.block_trace_vectors()` gives the per-block trace functionals, which `project_phi_to_slk` reads when it is passed the group (the model's `pos_phi_project_slk` path and the semantics extractor now pass the group). Every local-basis read rounds through the stored basis dtype first, so it equals `generators.to(dtype)` entry for entry and the earlier float32 numerics are unchanged. A spy on the generator descriptor shows that a training step and the evaluation diagnostics of a tiny model of each of the three groups, with a learned positional frame and `pos_phi_project_slk=True`, no longer read the dense basis at all; before the change both did.

`tests/test_block_local_codec_20261016.py` (16 tests) checks that construction at K=256, H=8 builds no dense basis, that every local-basis path equals the dense basis in float32 and float64 without materializing it, that the compact transport matches the dense-basis route to float64 rounding, the certificate under reading, moving and in-place mutation, and the descriptor spy above. The 30 existing test files that touch these groups, the head-block basis or the trace projection pass, except `test_amp_routes_20261012.py::test_cuda_autocast_probe_matches_the_rule`, which needs CUDA and fails identically on unmodified `main` with CUDA hidden.

## SU(N) irrep towers (gauge-group buildout, su_n M1)

`gauge_group="su_n"` gives SU(N), N = `group_n` >= 3, acting on the belief space through a direct sum of real irreps, one irrep block per head, tied by default like `so_n`. `vfe3/geometry/generators.py` gains `generate_sun(N)`, the generalized Gell-Mann basis X_a = i lambda_a (complex128, (N^2 - 1, N, N)), ordered so that its first N(N-1)/2 elements are exactly `generate_son`'s L_jk, followed by the symmetric i(E_jk + E_kj) and the N - 1 diagonal elements; every X_a is anti-Hermitian and traceless with tr(X_a^H X_b) = 2 delta_ab. `realify` maps X = A + iB to [[A, -B], [B, A]], a Lie-algebra homomorphism that sends anti-Hermitian matrices to skew ones, and `generate_sun_real` is the realified basis, a faithful real representation of su(N) on which the irrep registry, the homomorphism check, the Gram certificate and the basis fingerprint run unchanged.

`vfe3/geometry/irreps.py` gains arity-2 irrep labels (`register_irrep(..., arity=2)`, label grammar '<prefix><p>_<q>'; every arity-1 family and label parses exactly as before) and the family `su:pq`, the irrep of highest weight (p, q). Its real dimension is the complex dimension C(p+N-1, p) C(q+N-1, q) - C(p+N-2, p-1) C(q+N-2, q-1) for the real type p = q and twice that for the complex type; labels with p < q are refused, since (q, p) is R-equivalent to (p, q). Built so far are the trivial 'pq0_0' (no generators) and the adjoint 'pq1_1', rho(X_a)_cb = f_abc. The structure constants are the exact projections f_abc = <R_c, [R_a, R_b]>_F / ||R_c||_F^2 on the Frobenius-orthogonal realified basis, computed with deterministic products: the first version took them from the existing least-squares solve, and two identical builds then produced different basis fingerprints, which would make an su_n checkpoint unloadable. The adjoint images are antisymmetrized to remove rounding, so they are exactly skew. Every other (p, q) has its dimension but is refused at config time until the realification of M2.

`vfe3/geometry/groups.py` factors the tower construction (direct sum, tying, Gram certificate, fingerprint) out of `_build_so_n` into `_build_irrep_tower`, shared by `so_n` and the new `_build_su_n`; the `so_n` basis fingerprints of three towers recorded on unmodified main are reproduced exactly. `su_n` registers the head-block invariant families, traits algebra "su" with both tyings (native "shared") and no pair rotations, and no pullback capability (fails closed). Config validates `gauge_group` against the registry, so the new group needs no list edit; it refuses `irrep_spec="random"` and `use_cg_coupling` for su_n (the random sampler draws so(N) towers and CG's defining representation is so-only), the strict-pullback budget estimate counts N^2 - 1 generators for su_n, and `su_n` joins the frame-condition inert groups (its frames are orthogonal). The `gauge_group`, `gauge_tying`, `group_n` and `irrep_spec` comments of `vfe3/config.py` and `train_vfe3.py` list the new group and its labels.

`tests/test_sun_irreps_20261016.py` (18 tests) checks the basis (anti-Hermitian, traceless, Gram 2I, so(N) slice, real structure constants, realification a homomorphism), the dimensions against the SU(3) Weyl formula and the SU(4), SU(5) adjoints, the adjoint block for N = 3 and 4 (exactly skew, homomorphism, Casimir -sum rho^2 = 4N I, real commutant of dimension 1), the builder (Gram certificate 24 at SU(3) with two adjoints, admissibility with the diagonal family as negative control, tying, a pinned deterministic fingerprint), config acceptance and refusals, the so_n fingerprints, train steps and the gauge-equivariance residual on three projected routes under both tyings, and a float64 finite-difference phi gradient on two routes. The 70 test files that touch irreps, so_n, tying, CG or config pass (1,693 tests).

## SU(N) irrep towers: every (p, q) and CG coupling (gauge-group buildout, su_n M2)

`su_n` now builds every irrep (p, q) with p >= q and p + q <= 6 (the tensor-power cost guard of so_n). `_su_tensor_irrep` works on the traceless tensors of Sym^p (x) Sym^q: the product of the two monomial bases is real and so is the contraction of a fundamental with an antifundamental slot, so the invariant subspace has a real orthonormal basis Q from the same ordered Gram-Schmidt as so(N), and the complex images are rho_C(X) = Q^T L(X) Q with X acting on the p fundamental slots and its conjugate on the q antifundamental slots. For p != q (complex type) the real form is the realification [[A, -B], [B, A]], of dimension 2D. For p = q (real type) the antilinear J = P o conj, with P the exchange of the fundamental and antifundamental slots, commutes with the action and squares to one; in the Q coordinates it is a -> M conj(a) with M = Q^T P Q a real symmetric involution, and the +1 eigenvectors of M together with i times its -1 eigenvectors form a real orthonormal basis of dimension D in which the images are real. The build raises on an imaginary residual, a skew residual, or a Casimir that differs from 4 C_2(p, q) I, with C_2 = [(p^2 + q^2)(N - 1)/N + 2pq/N + (p + q)(N - 1)]/2 (`su_casimir`). The adjoint keeps its M1 construction, so the pinned fingerprint of adjoint towers is unchanged. `vfe3.geometry.cg` gains the su branch of its defining representation (the realified basis), so the Clebsch-Gordan solve and `CGCoupling` work for su_n towers and config no longer refuses `use_cg_coupling` for su_n. SU(3) 8 (x) 8 -> 8 has real multiplicity 2, the antisymmetric f and the symmetric d coupling; the solver returns an arbitrary basis of that two-dimensional null space (as documented in `cg.py`), and a self-pair keeps exactly the slots with a d component, which is a live quadratic self-channel that the SO(3) l1 tower lacks. The config and launcher comments list the general labels.

`tests/test_sun_irreps_m2_20261016.py` (21 tests) checks the SU(3) irreps pq1_0, pq2_0, pq2_1, pq2_2 and pq3_0 (dimensions 6, 12, 30, 27, 20; exact skew; homomorphism; Casimir; real commutant of dimension 2 for the complex type and 1 for the real type), the Casimir values, the centre Z_3 acting as omega^(p - q) through the exponential of the central element, the SU(4) fundamental and the size guard, a tower fingerprint identical across processes, the CG multiplicities and the self-pair rule, config acceptance of mixed towers and CG, train steps and the gauge-equivariance residual on a mixed [pq1_0, pq1_1] tower under both tyings and both projected routes, a CG-coupled adjoint tower, and a finite-difference phi gradient. The M1 test file now accepts the fundamental and CG. Under two and four threads both su_n test files pass, and the 71 test files that touch irreps, so_n, su_n, CG or config pass (1,714 tests). A later related run exposed that the real-type (2, 2) images changed in the last bit at four threads (threaded matmul reduction order), so the tower fingerprint depended on the thread count; `_su_tensor_irrep` now builds under a single-thread guard that restores the caller's count, and the fingerprint test compares one and four threads. The so_n tensor irreps checked (l2 and l3 up to N = 7, l4 at N = 4) are bit-identical across thread counts and are unchanged.

## block_sl, block_borel and block_unipotent (gauge-group buildout, Plan C)

Three block-local groups join `block_so`, `block_co` and `block_glmc`, each built by `_build_block_local` from a Frobenius-orthonormal local basis, so they store only the (m, d, d) local basis and reuse the compact head-block transport, the codec and the pullback M-step (`pullback_per_block`). `block_sl` is SL(d)^H with the local basis `local_basis_sl` (off-diagonal E_ij, then the Helmert traceless diagonals), n_gen = H (d^2 - 1). Every generator is traceless, so every frame has det 1 per head, the trace projection behind `pos_phi_project_slk` is the identity (its trace vectors vanish), and the -c_i log|det g_i| shift of the receiver energy under a gauge move is zero. It contains every pair rotation, so RoPE stays inside it, and it is the third rung of the ladder SO -> CO -> SL -> GL. The frame condition bound `phi_frame_condition_max` now accepts it: the bound replaces the traceless symmetric part S_0 of a head block by a traceless S_0', so the change lies in sl(d). `block_borel` is B+(d)^H (upper triangular with positive diagonal, `local_basis_borel`, n_gen = H d(d+1)/2), on which exp is a diffeomorphism; it preserves each head's coordinate flag and acts simply transitively on SPD matrices through the Cholesky factor, and with `block_so` it reaches the identity component of `block_glk` by the Iwasawa factorization. `block_unipotent` is N+(d)^H (unit diagonal, the new `local_basis_unipotent`, n_gen = H d(d-1)/2), whose exponential is a polynomial; d = 3 is the Heisenberg group. Neither triangular group contains a rotation, so the config warns that frame-space RoPE leaves them, and the frame condition bound refuses them because the symmetric projection does not keep b+(d) or n+(d). All three declare the full and head-block Gaussian families invariant. `projected_gauge_equivariance` now also reports `log_det_shift_max`, the largest volume correction it added to the receiver energy. Config and launcher comments list the new groups, and the strict-pullback budget knows their algebra dimensions.

`tests/test_block_sl_borel_20261016.py` (33 tests) checks the local bases (orthonormal, traceless or triangular), structure and bracket closure, exponentials landing in each group (det 1, triangular, unit or positive diagonal, the polynomial exponential of n+), the identity trace projection, admissibility with the diagonal family as negative control, config acceptance, refusals and the RoPE warning, the frame condition bound on `block_sl`, train steps and the gauge-equivariance residual on three projected routes with a zero log-det shift for the det-1 groups and a nonzero one for `block_borel`, a pullback-group train step, a RoPE train step on `block_sl`, and a float64 finite-difference phi gradient on two routes. `tests/test_audit_20261012_frames.py` runs its two projection tests on `block_sl` as well. The 56 test files that touch the block-local groups, the group registry, the frame condition bound, the equivariance extract or config give 1,293 passed and 9 failed under four threads; the failures are the CUDA autocast probe and the eight scipy t-quantile cases that also fail on main.

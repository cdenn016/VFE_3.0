## Model and returned state

`PrototypeAgentBlockMLP` has three arrays: `nu` with shape `(M,K)`, `log_lambda` with shape `(M,K)`, and `log_pi` with shape `(M,)`, where `M=expansion*K`. They define diagonal Gaussian components and a normalized categorical prior. There is no separate value matrix. See [parameter registration](<C:/Users/chris and christine/Desktop/VFE_3.0/vfe3/model/block_mlp.py:1687>). The exact helper computes normalized label probabilities and posterior moments from Gaussian overlap scores, accumulating within-component covariance and between-template spread; it returns aggregate statistics rather than a persistent array of conditional Gaussian components. See [posterior result types](<C:/Users/chris and christine/Desktop/VFE_3.0/vfe3/model/prototype_agents.py:82>), [tile calculations](<C:/Users/chris and christine/Desktop/VFE_3.0/vfe3/model/prototype_agents.py:278>), and [moment assembly](<C:/Users/chris and christine/Desktop/VFE_3.0/vfe3/model/prototype_agents.py:431>).

There is one categorical label over the entire state. Compact head blocks change the covariance representation and factorization size; they do not create independent labels per head. The component score sums contributions from all blocks before applying the template softmax [block scoring](<C:/Users/chris and christine/Desktop/VFE_3.0/vfe3/model/prototype_agents.py:305>).

The block operates after attention, optional coupling, and the configured block normalization. Its record is therefore the immediate working mean, which can already include normalization [caller](<C:/Users/chris and christine/Desktop/VFE_3.0/vfe3/model/block.py:229>). `forward_moments` applies inference, optional EM capture, and dropout on the mean increment, then returns only the new mean and selected covariance [forward implementation](<C:/Users/chris and christine/Desktop/VFE_3.0/vfe3/model/block_mlp.py:1756>).

| Choice | Actual propagation |
|---|---|
| `exact` with `posterior` | Exact posterior mean and covariance projected to the supported representation; the mixture itself is compressed. |
| `mean_field` with `posterior` | Gaussian coordinate from the last CAVI sweep; no between-template spread. |
| Either inference with `passthrough` | Updated mean with the incoming covariance, which generally differs from posterior moment matching. |
| Nonzero training dropout | Dropout modifies the mean increment after inference; the returned mean generally differs from the deterministic posterior mean. |

For a diagonal input, the exact helper returns diagonal covariance entries. For compact blocks it returns diagonal blocks. Dense input permits the full posterior covariance [assembly](<C:/Users/chris and christine/Desktop/VFE_3.0/vfe3/model/prototype_agents.py:438>). The reference-coordinate caller pulls the belief into reference coordinates and pushes the result back through the realized frame; local diagonal storage then projects again [reference caller](<C:/Users/chris and christine/Desktop/VFE_3.0/vfe3/model/block.py:427>). The geometry memo describes the resulting covariance restriction.

`BeliefState` stores means, covariances, and frames, with no prototype-label field [state definition](<C:/Users/chris and christine/Desktop/VFE_3.0/vfe3/belief.py:15>). Responsibilities affect the returned moments, gradients, and diagnostics but are not carried as a persistent model-belief channel. Diagnostic hooks recompute template statistics [hooks](<C:/Users/chris and christine/Desktop/VFE_3.0/vfe3/train.py:3353>).

## Finite mean-field inference

The mean-field helper initializes label inference at the incoming moments. A sweep updates labels by the Gaussian reverse-KL score and then forms the Gaussian power posterior. It always uses the original incoming `(xi,C)` in the Gaussian coordinate. It does not repeatedly replace that prior by its previous posterior. See [CAVI loop](<C:/Users/chris and christine/Desktop/VFE_3.0/vfe3/model/prototype_agents.py:639>).

The final return contains the labels used to produce the final Gaussian; there is no extra label refresh at the end [return](<C:/Users/chris and christine/Desktop/VFE_3.0/vfe3/model/prototype_agents.py:680>). Thus a finite number of sweeps does not establish stationarity. The implementation can measure label staleness by comparing this row with a new label coordinate at the returned Gaussian [diagnostic](<C:/Users/chris and christine/Desktop/VFE_3.0/vfe3/model/block_mlp.py:1903>). Zero staleness alone does not establish a global variational optimum, as the symmetric example in the probabilistic memo shows. The loop remains differentiable through its sweeps.

## What the ELBO diagnostic evaluates

`prototype_elbo` evaluates a product recognition law consisting of one Gaussian and one categorical row. It does not accept a collection of label-conditioned Gaussian laws. Its expression is expected template log likelihood plus categorical entropy minus the KL from the Gaussian to the incoming Gaussian [ELBO implementation](<C:/Users/chris and christine/Desktop/VFE_3.0/vfe3/model/prototype_agents.py:717>).

In exact mode, `elbo_and_evidence` passes the moment-matched Gaussian and the exact template weights into that product-law calculation [diagnostic caller](<C:/Users/chris and christine/Desktop/VFE_3.0/vfe3/model/block_mlp.py:1863>). The resulting bound can be strictly below the exact evidence even when the posterior helper computes its intended quantities accurately. This is a distinction between the two scored laws, not evidence of a failed Gaussian solve.

The diagnostic requests posterior covariance even when the propagated block uses passthrough, recomputes on detached float64 inputs, and excludes dropout. It therefore does not necessarily score the Gaussian passed to the next block. It is excluded from the training loss. The separate optional EM objective has different semantics.

## Learning and sharing

Exact-mode EM uses detached component-conditioned moments and exact responsibilities. Mean-field EM uses the final detached Gaussian and label coordinates. The live bank parameters remain differentiable [EM entry point](<C:/Users/chris and christine/Desktop/VFE_3.0/vfe3/model/block_mlp.py:1794>), [component statistic calculation](<C:/Users/chris and christine/Desktop/VFE_3.0/vfe3/model/prototype_agents.py:500>). The model sums the captured terms over layers and scored tokens, subtracting their weighted average per scored token from the supervised loss [loss assembly](<C:/Users/chris and christine/Desktop/VFE_3.0/vfe3/model/model.py:1494>).

This path uses gradients of an expected complete-data log likelihood. It does not assign closed-form EM estimates to the arrays. Ordinary downstream prediction gradients also reach the bank through its forward computation. Training still invokes backward [training step](<C:/Users/chris and christine/Desktop/VFE_3.0/vfe3/train.py:916>). The templates occupy optimizer groups with the configured block learning rate [groups](<C:/Users/chris and christine/Desktop/VFE_3.0/vfe3/train.py:337>). Detaching the auxiliary EM statistics does not make the whole learner backpropagation free, and an auxiliary internal-record objective does not establish predictive credit from observed words through a separate local generative learner.

A separate block module is constructed for every layer [construction](<C:/Users/chris and christine/Desktop/VFE_3.0/vfe3/model/model.py:421>), and the stack selects it by layer index [stack](<C:/Users/chris and christine/Desktop/VFE_3.0/vfe3/model/stack.py:132>). Within a layer, all token occurrences and batches use the same arrays. The bank stays fixed during an ordinary forward pass. It contains `2MK+M` parameters. These are point parameters, not posterior random variables; a shared learned bank is not a hyperprior over its own means and covariances.

Working-variance initialization is implemented separately from training: it sets a common initial variance from incoming covariance and a chosen initial pull, and samples template means from a calibration batch [initialization](<C:/Users/chris and christine/Desktop/VFE_3.0/vfe3/model/block_mlp.py:1727>). The model calibrates successive banks through already calibrated layers [model routine](<C:/Users/chris and christine/Desktop/VFE_3.0/vfe3/model/model.py:1003>).

## The adjacent template block and vocabulary banks

`GaussianTemplateBlockMLP` uses a similar reverse-KL label score but adds `responsibilities @ values` through an independent value matrix. Its covariance passes through [template block](<C:/Users/chris and christine/Desktop/VFE_3.0/vfe3/model/block_mlp.py:1509>). It has `3MK+M` parameters. The prototype block instead derives its update direction from Gaussian conditioning. Consequently the attached note's affine-logit analogy, quoted from `gaussian_template`, must not be read as an identity between the two modes.

The encoder selects the observed word's prior and frame by lookup [encoding](<C:/Users/chris and christine/Desktop/VFE_3.0/vfe3/model/prior_bank.py:2185>). The KL decoder compares the held final belief with word templates, using a divergence temperature and an optional weighted corpus prior [decoder](<C:/Users/chris and christine/Desktop/VFE_3.0/vfe3/model/prior_bank.py:1246>). At effective temperature one and with the declared prior, the Gaussian-KL score is a mean-field label coordinate. It is not the overlap score used by exact prototype inference, and it is not followed by the prototype's Gaussian state update.

Encoder and decoder banks can be tied or separately trained after an initial clone [bank creation](<C:/Users/chris and christine/Desktop/VFE_3.0/vfe3/model/prior_bank.py:898>). This does not tie either bank to an intermediate prototype bank. The vocabulary prior is a smoothed corpus frequency buffer [prior update](<C:/Users/chris and christine/Desktop/VFE_3.0/vfe3/model/prior_bank.py:1003>); prototype prior weights are learned parameters. The three mechanisms admit a common presentation/evaluator vocabulary but do not establish a single internal latent generative model.

## Current choices and available paths

The live launcher uses `K=128`, four layers, `gaussian_diagonal`, kernel predictive readout, local block and decoder coordinates, an enabled coordinate GELU block, affine LayerNorm, zero block dropout, and block learning rate `0.005`. The decoder is `diagonal_chunked`, with temperature `0.1`, unigram weight one, and an untied bank. Prototype settings are inactive: mean-field inference, one CAVI sweep, working-variance initialization, and zero EM weight. These choices are intentional working state. See [launcher architecture](<C:/Users/chris and christine/Desktop/VFE_3.0/train_vfe3.py:115>), [block settings](<C:/Users/chris and christine/Desktop/VFE_3.0/train_vfe3.py:191>), and [learning rate](<C:/Users/chris and christine/Desktop/VFE_3.0/train_vfe3.py:340>). The October 20 note's `K=256` is an older snapshot.

Prototype mode is registered and supports exact or mean-field inference with passthrough or posterior covariance [factory](<C:/Users/chris and christine/Desktop/VFE_3.0/vfe3/model/block_mlp.py:2045>). It does not require the attention readout itself to be `minimal_agent`. Reference-coordinate prototype processing is admitted under the compatible projected-predictive configurations [validation](<C:/Users/chris and christine/Desktop/VFE_3.0/vfe3/config.py:2140>). Full covariance supports the complete local-frame congruence. `gaussian_block` restricts exact prototype posterior covariance because between-template spread can couple blocks [validation](<C:/Users/chris and christine/Desktop/VFE_3.0/vfe3/config.py:1525>). `gaussian_frame_diagonal` currently admits only coordinate block MLPs [validation](<C:/Users/chris and christine/Desktop/VFE_3.0/vfe3/config.py:1564>). These are source-traced paths, not configurations newly trained here.

## Historical evidence and its limits

The [October 9 report](<C:/Users/chris and christine/Desktop/VFE_3.0/docs/experiments/2026-10-09_prototype-K128-workvar-collapse.md>) records a collapsed long run and a subsequent short, single-seed 2-by-2 comparison. Both learning-rate `0.05` arms collapsed; neither `0.005` arm did within 5,000 steps. Working-variance initialization at the lower rate retained more gain. The follow-up corrects the earlier causal account: responsibilities concentrated before the prior entropy collapsed. It does not establish that `log_pi` initiated the failure. The checkpoints were not reloaded in this investigation.

Those runs establish behavior in their recorded configurations. They do not settle performance against the current GELU baseline. The reported effective-template statistic is the mean over positions of `exp(H(r_i))` [diagnostic](<C:/Users/chris and christine/Desktop/VFE_3.0/vfe3/model/block_mlp.py:1930>). It measures routing diffuseness within a position. A bank of identical templates can have high diffuseness without useful specialization. Population entropy, conditional entropy, their mutual-information difference, and predictive contribution must be distinguished, as already explained in the [October 20 investigation](<C:/Users/chris and christine/Desktop/VFE_3.0/docs/research/2026-10-20-neural-free-continuation/prototypes.md>).

## Remediation, first batch

The decode-side mean tables gained their own decay, `decode_mu_weight_decay` (default `None`, which inherits `mu_weight_decay` and so changes no existing run). It applies to the untied `decode_mu_embed` and the linear readout weight only, is inert on a tied prior-bank decode, and is exposed in the `train_vfe3.py` launcher. It answers audit item 2: re-scoring the 135 test windows that hold every target with training count at most 30 showed that in 36.41 the ten targets whose type never occurs in training cost about 1,760 nats each, and that the 2,319 zero-count decode rows were still moving outward at the end of training (median norm 42.1 at step 90k, 44.5 at 120k) under Adam second moments near 1e-10, with nothing pulling them back. Pricing the 32 targets with count at most 3 at their unigram cost gives test perplexity 33.88 for 36.41 and 32.63 for 32.65. `inactive_fields` now also marks `weight_decay` when every optimizer group sets its own decay and `phi_weight_decay` under the manifold frame updates, and its `windowed` reason names the uniform prior it equals.

A float64 model now decodes in float64: the decode and CE seams of `VFEModel` upcast reduced-precision beliefs to float32 and leave float64 unchanged, where `.float()` had rounded them and capped whole-model fp64 finite-difference checks at about 1e-3. The two families' fp64 losses in `tests/test_remediation_20260923_A.py` now agree to one ulp instead of bitwise, since the bitwise agreement came from that rounding. The generic log-partition oracle is exact (`FullGaussian.natural` drops its ridge; fp64 agreement 2.2e-15), float64 so_n models carry the float64 irrep basis, the log-variance overflow warning and the compact chart-radius check ride the scorer's deferred host read (two fewer synchronizations per step), and a float64 source recovery no longer writes into the factor autograd saved. Finalization adds a resumed checkpoint's wall clock to `wall_time_s` and `device_hours` (and records it as `wall_time_resumed_s`), the channel decomposition has a `no_rope` arm under RoPE, the context-sensitivity record reads the final belief (with the layer-0 value kept as `layer0_readout`), and the three analysis entrypoints read their configuration dictionaries only.

In the training loop, a failed log-step diagnostic fills the established columns with NaN instead of aborting at the next metrics write, a failed `best_model.pt` publication is counted and retried at the next improving evaluation, `val_attn_collapsed_heads` counts heads whose mean normalized entropy over the diagnostic windows is below one half, `train()` and `evaluate()` refuse a VFE model whose attention prior is not causal for next-token scoring, and the self-loop spectral radius is evaluated on a fixed 256-token subsample (15.1 s to 0.94 s per layer call at B=32, N=128, K=128 on the CPU; the maximum is now a lower bound over tokens, exact on the subsample and on tokens with every self weight equal to one).

In the projected scorer, the healthy key factor has a closed-form backward (forward bitwise identical; 232 to 36 ms on 4096 SPD 32×32 blocks on the CPU, flat on CUDA), the diagonal-family projection contracts through the source root (CUDA paper shape at B=32: 29.1 to 26.6 ms under "highest", 27.1 to 26.3 ms under "high"; live shape 6.4 to 3.9 ms), a tied comparison map factors its covariance once, and the factor-route pair-KL tile runs at IEEE fp32. ## Remediation, second batch

The user kept both scorer optimizations, so `tests/data/equal_head_goldens_20261005.pt` was regenerated under the CUDA interpreter on the CPU device with one thread (torch 2.10.0.dev20251210+cu128, now stored as a string). Against the previous file the forwards are bitwise equal on block_glk and on the so_n full routes, the so_n diagonal forwards moved at the float32 rounding level, and gradients moved by at most 3.7e-7 relative on block_glk and 1.2e-4 on so_n; the test passes under both interpreters. The `canonical_frame` block MLP is now on the gauge-pure path (audit item 42): it applies the same realized-frame dressing U_i F(U_i^{-1} x) as the reference-coordinate blocks, which were already counted as covariant, and the pin in `tests/test_gauge_block_mlp_reporting.py` now asserts that. The exponential content chart is evaluated in product form, O' = e^{OT} T^{-1}, with one K by K `matrix_exp` in place of the 2K by 2K augmented exponential of the φ₁ form (item 31). On the RTX 5090 at K = 128 over four layers a float64 forward and backward of the charts took 6.7 ms against 17.7 ms, at K = 20 the two were equal (launch bound), and the two forms agree to 3.2e-14 relative.

The block LayerNorm can now carry the covariance (item 9). Under `block_norm_covariance="pushforward"` every block-norm seam in local coordinates replaces Σ by its first-order pushforward J_LN Σ J_LN^T + f D Σ D, with D = diag(γ)/s and f = `block_mlp_covariance_floor`; the diagonal family reads the diagonal of that matrix, evaluated in closed form at O(K) per token without forming J (`vfe3/model/block_mlp.py`, `layernorm_diagonal_pushforward`). The option is inert under `block_mlp_covariance="delta_full"`, whose Jacobian already composes the norm's. `delta_full` itself is now admitted on the diagonal family through the same M-projection, (J∘J)σ plus the floor, with no Cholesky factorization; gauge_gate keeps refusing it there. Posthoc rows 10 and 11 report not applicable under the pushforward, and `inactive_fields` no longer calls a full-family run value-identical to the diagonal family when the pushforward densifies Σ.

The canonical free energy is available as an opt-in log-step diagnostic (item 12). Under `diagnose_canonical_free_energy=True` each layer's diagnostics evaluate, at the captured beliefs, Σ_i [α_i KL(q_i‖p_i) + R(α_i)] + λ_β Σ_h Σ_ij [β*_ij D^h_ij + τ β*_ij log(β*_ij/π_ij)], where D^h_ij is the KL between the head-h marginals of N(ξ_i, C_i) and N(ξ_j, C_j) in reference coordinates (the energy of the retired transported route), β* = softmax(log π − D/τ) is the canonical router on the model's causal support, τ is the layer's routing temperature and α the registered self-coupling. The values fill the legacy `self_coupling`, `self_divergence`, `belief_coupling`, `attention_entropy` and total columns as per-token means, and `canonical_beta_tv` records the mean total-variation distance between the model's router and β*. The routing block costs 0.04 s at B = 64, N = 128, K = 128 with eight heads on the GPU. With the flag off no work is done and every other diagnostic is bitwise unchanged.

The `channel_likelihood` receiver of `projected_gradient` gained the dual measurement (item 11). Under `relational_receiver_measurement="inverse"` head h measures ξ_i through the head-h rows of O'^{-1}, inverted once per context build, instead of through O'_h^T. In float64 with equal channel precisions and α → 0 its frozen-context minimizer equals the kernel mean to 2.2e-13, where the adjoint misses it by 23.3, and under (U h, T h, S h, h^{-1}O') it moves by at most 1.7e-12 against 0.7 to 121 for the adjoint. The default `"adjoint"` is bitwise unchanged.

Both structured mean-field models can now run several coordinate-ascent sweeps (item 23): `minimal_agent_cavi_steps` relabels the records at the posterior query moments (the definition of `_label_staleness`, now one shared helper), and `block_mlp_cavi_steps` recomputes the prototype responsibilities at the previous sweep's posterior and redoes the power posterior from the prior moments. Over twenty sweeps the label staleness fell from 0.15 to 0.65 to between 1.7e-15 and 2.6e-6 on the test cases, at per-sweep rates of 0.15 to 0.56; one receiver plateaued near 6e-3 for thirty sweeps before converging, so a few sweeps are not guaranteed to reach the fixed point. `block_mlp_em_weight` adds the EM M-step objective of the prototype templates to the loss, −w mean_i Σ_a r̄_ia [log π̂_a + E_q log N(ξ; ν_a, λ_a)] with detached responsibilities and block-input moments, so it trains only (ν, λ, π) (item 24). One sweep and zero weight are bitwise unchanged.

The type-prior tables gained a natural-gradient M-step (item 22), separate from the frame table's `fisher_group`. Under `m_table_update_mode="fisher"` (`vfe3/table_optim.py`) each row of `mu_embed` and `sigma_log_embed` moves by −η (N/n_v)(s_v ∂μ, 2 ∂l), the gradient preconditioned by the count-weighted Gaussian Fisher metric (n_v/N) diag(1/s_v, 1/2), with each row's step bounded in Fisher norm by `m_table_fisher_trust_radius` (so the per-step KL of p_v is at most r²/2) and scaled by the schedule factor lr_t/lr_0 of its AdamW group, whose decoupled decay is applied unchanged. At η = 1 one step on a self-coupling objective lands on the closed-form M-step mean to 1e-12. The validator refuses a prior-bank decoder that reads these tables, since its softmax gradient reaches rows with n_v = 0 that the metric does not describe, and the block-Cholesky table; the step composes with both manifold phi optimizers, and `metrics.csv` gains four `table_fisher_*` columns under the mode. The compact head-block frame route for the other block groups (item 26) was explained to the user and not implemented.

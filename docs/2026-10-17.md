# October 17, 2026

## Removal of the BCH composition stack (audit 2026-10-16, F91)

The positional frame composes as the exact group product exp(X_i) exp(Y_n), the `right_phi` factor of `build_factored_transport`, so the Baker-Campbell-Hausdorff machinery in `vfe3/geometry/lie_ops.py` had no production caller. The composition registry (`_COMPOSE`, `register_compose`, `get_compose`, `compose_euclidean`, `compose_phi`), `compose_bch` with its Dynkin series `_bch_dynkin_correction`, the group-product residual gate `_fail_if_bch_residual_exceeds`, the block gather `_equal_diag_blocks`, and the bracket-closure guard `require_basis_closed` with the helpers and bounded LRU caches only it used (`_bracket_closure_residual`, `_spans_matrix_algebra`, `_direct_sum_blocks`, `_basis_value_signature`, `_cached_basis_value_signature`, `_bounded_lru_store` and the `_BRACKET_CLOSURE_*` tables) were deleted, about 490 lines. `GaugeGroup.gram_pinv` and its two cache fields went with them, since nothing in production called the method. The coordinate maps `embed_phi`, `extract_phi` and `gram_pinv`, the bracket helpers, `CompactBlockElement`, `project_phi_to_slk` and `_block_trace_vectors` remain, and so does `_from_equal_diag_blocks`, which `CompactBlockElement.to_dense` uses. The separate strict pullback closure certificate in `vfe3/geometry/phi_preconditioner.py` is untouched. The module docstring now describes what the module provides. Tests that exercised only the deleted code were removed: the BCH and composition tests of `tests/test_lie_ops.py`, the `register_compose` duplicate-key test, the compose tests of the compact block transport file (whose remaining tests now carry a local `_equal_diag_blocks` helper), the blocked-versus-dense `compose_bch` test, the non-closed-basis `compose_bch` test, the `gram_pinv` cache assertions, `tests/test_basis_closure_blocks_20261009.py` as a whole, and the two `require_basis_closed` tests of the strict-closure file.

## KL-only divergence seam (audit 2026-10-16, F93)

The retired `divergence_family` and `renyi_order` fields (whose migration in `config_from_serialized` is unchanged) left the KL, the Renyi divergence at alpha = 1, as the divergence of every route. The squared Hellinger, Bhattacharyya and Jeffreys functionals and the per-coordinate Bhattacharyya and Jeffreys forms were deleted from `vfe3/families/base.py` together with their registrations and their re-exports in `vfe3/divergence.py`. `renyi`, `renyi_per_coord`, `_renyi_from_log_partition` and the diagonal, full and block closed forms in `vfe3/families/gaussian.py` keep their `alpha` keyword so call signatures stay stable, but each now evaluates only its former KL branch and raises `ValueError` when |alpha - 1| is not below `_KL_ALPHA_SWITCH`, through one helper `_require_kl_order`. The blend branches, the `_RENYI_KL_BAND` float64 islands, `_warn_alpha_gt_one` and the alpha > 1 indefinite-blend Cholesky gate are gone. The guard message of `free_energy.self_divergence_per_coord`, the docstring of the invariance verifier `check_admissible`, the prior-bank comment on the reference decode and the module docstrings were updated. The numpy scorers in `vfe3/semantics/` are analysis code and were left alone.

The KL path was checked to be bitwise unchanged. Before the deletion a scratch script recorded, at K = 4 with three batch entries in float32 and float64, the values and the gradients with respect to both means and both covariances of `kl()`, `renyi(alpha=1)`, `renyi_per_coord(alpha=1)`, the family closed forms and per-coordinate methods, and the generic log-partition KL, for the diagonal, block and full families on a generic pair, a near-equal pair and (full family) an ill-conditioned pair with condition numbers near 1e6 and 1e7. After the deletion all 420 recorded tensors compared equal under `torch.equal`. Tests that exercised only the removed functionals or alpha != 1 were deleted, and mixed tests were reduced to their alpha = 1 cases, in `tests/test_divergence.py`, `tests/test_eigvalsh_batch_ceiling_20260807.py` (the `safe_eigvalsh` and `safe_eigh` tests remain), `tests/test_families.py`, `tests/test_full_covariance.py`, `tests/test_fullcov_alpha_roadmap_2026_06_13.py`, `tests/test_float64_dtype_policy_20260712.py` and `tests/test_prior_bank.py`; the full-family refusal test in `tests/test_full_gaussian_coordinate_kl.py` now matches the new message. `tests/test_cleanup_20261017_kl_only.py` checks that every Renyi entry point refuses orders outside the KL switch for all three families, that orders inside it return the KL exactly, and that the removed names are absent from `vfe3.divergence` and its registries.

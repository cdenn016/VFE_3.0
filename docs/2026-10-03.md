## Triangle holonomy of the head-sum link field

This implements the measurement proposed in the link_curvature note (Research vault, `manuscripts/Attention/link_curvature.tex`). The projected routes apply the reference-coordinate edge operator $\hat O B_{ij}\hat T$ with $B_{ij}=\bigoplus_h\beta^h_{ij}I_d$. With tiled heads ($Hd=K$), the triangle holonomy $F_{ijk}=L_{ik}^{-1}L_{ij}L_{jk}$ on a strict triangle $k<j<i$ is conjugate to $R_{ijk}G$. Here $G=\hat T\hat O$ is the routing-independent head-transfer matrix and $R_{ijk}=\bigoplus_h\rho^h_{ijk}I_d$ with $\rho^h_{ijk}=\beta^h_{ij}\beta^h_{jk}/\beta^h_{ik}$. The frames drop out, and the routing factor is flat exactly for $\beta_{ij}=c_i/c_j$.

`vfe3/metrics.py` adds `routing_triangle_defect_sums`, which returns the exact path-weighted moments $\sum w$, $\sum wx$ and $\sum wx^2$ of $x=\log\rho^h_{ijk}$ with $w=\beta_{ij}\beta_{jk}$, by six matrix products and no $N^3$ tensor. It also adds `triangle_holonomy_eigenvalues`, the spectra of $R_{ijk}G$.

Training (`vfe3/train.py`): every evaluation now appends `diagnostics/routing_holonomy.csv`, with one row per (layer, head). The rows hold the mean path mass per query row and the path-weighted mean and RMS of $\log\rho$, for the learned router and for its prior alone. They are computed in the existing capture pass on the fixed diagnostic windows, with no extra forward. A failure is counted as `routing_holonomy` and does not disturb the other diagnostics. Earlier runs cannot be backfilled, because their map snapshots hold no embeddings.

Post hoc (`vfe3/posthoc/holonomy.py`, run as `python -m vfe3.posthoc.holonomy RUN_DIR`): for each full checkpoint, this computes the same moments on evenly spaced validation windows. It also computes the curvature size $\kappa=(\frac1K\sum_k|\mathrm{Log}\lambda_k|^2)^{1/2}$ of $R_{ijk}G$, $G$ and $R_{ijk}$, on triangles sampled in proportion to the head-mean path weight. It further reports the spectrum invariants of $G$ at every map snapshot.

Tests: `tests/test_routing_holonomy_20261003.py` compares the moments with brute force, checks zero defect for $c_i/c_j$ routing and a nonzero defect for uniform causal routing, and checks the conjugacy against the direct product of edge operators. It also checks the training CSV and the observer. The new file and the three neighboring diagnostic test files passed (42 tests, CPU, one thread).

The post-hoc report also gives a scale-free $\kappa$, which removes the central scale $\frac1K\log|\det|$ and is zero for a scalar holonomy $cI$.

First measurements (CPU, 8 validation windows of 128 tokens, 256 sampled triangles per window and layer; one seed per run). In all three K=64 runs (54.73 depth-scaled, 54.39 identity, 46.42 projected_gradient), the scale-free $\kappa(G)$ starts at 0 at step 0. It rises to about 0.7–2.2 per layer and flattens toward the end of training, without a clear peak. In 46.42 the routing factor's median $\kappa(R)$ falls between 60k and 120k steps in five of six layers. The learned path-weighted RMS of $\log\rho$ is below the prior's 3.36 on most heads. Outputs: session scratchpad `holonomy_<run>.json`.

## Content-link charts (`content_link_chart`)

New field `content_link_chart` in `{"affine", "first_order", "exponential"}`, default `"affine"`. It selects the effective receiving maps $O'$ that the projected content link applies to the stored factors $(O, T)$; the parameters, their names and the optimizer groups are unchanged (AdamW). With tiled heads the stacked $T$ and $O$ are square, and the predictive message is $U_i\sum_jO'B_{ij}T\xi_j$, where $B_{ij}=\bigoplus_h\beta^h_{ij}I_d$. The three charts are:

- `"affine"`: $O'=O$, the current head sum, bitwise unchanged.
- `"first_order"`: $O'=T^{-1}+O$, the head-routed flat transport plus the head sum.
- `"exponential"`: $O'=T^{-1}+O\varphi_1(TO)=e^{OT}T^{-1}$. This is Option A's exponential link with shared generator $X=OT$ and routing outside the exponent.

In the exponential chart, $O'T=e^{OT}$ and the effective transfer $TO'=e^{TO}$ has positive determinant. The expansion of $O'$ in powers of the generator is: order zero $T^{-1}B_{ij}T=\sum_h\beta^h_{ij}P_h$, the head-routed flat transport; order one $OB_{ij}T$, the current head sum; and order $n$, $O(TO)^{n-1}B_{ij}T/n!$, cross-head round trips through $G=TO$. This reading is written up in `attention_unified.tex`, "The tiled message order by order".

Implementation:
- `vfe3/model/relational_operators.py` adds `chart_receive_map`. It evaluates the non-affine charts in float64. $\varphi_1$ comes from the top-right block of $\exp\begin{pmatrix}A&I\\0&0\end{pmatrix}$, with no inverse of $TO$.
- Every consumer reads the content maps through `content_factors()`. This covers the predictive mean, the moment-matched covariance, both receiver laws of `projected_gradient` (`vfe3/gradients/full_link.py`) and the link-spectrum diagnostics.
- Cost: the chart is evaluated once per layer per forward. The predictive mean and covariance share one evaluation, and each full-link context build makes one. `torch.linalg.inv_ex` avoids the host sync of `linalg.inv`; `matrix_exp` keeps one host sync per evaluation on CUDA (its degree selection reads a norm on the host).
- A singular $T$ gives non-finite maps rather than an error. The eval diagnostics now report `relational_content_T_min_singular_value`.
- Initialization: with $T=I$, the stored $O$ starts at $sI$, $(s-1)I$ or $(\log s)I$, so every chart starts at the content $sB_{ij}$.
- $O=0$ means zero content in the affine chart and flat transport in the other two. The content-map optimizer group has no weight decay, so nothing pulls toward either origin.
- The post-hoc map readers apply the chart when given the run config (`relational_maps(state, cfg)`, with the stored factor kept as `O_stored`). The affine balancedness drift `conserved_TO_drift` is NaN in the other charts.
- A later `"second_order"` truncation, $T^{-1}+O+\frac12O(TO)$, needs one branch in `chart_receive_map` and one initial value in `_chart_initial_output`.

Launchers: `train_vfe3.py` and `ablation.py` carry `content_link_chart = "affine"`. `ablation.py` adds the sweeps `content_link_chart` (three charts) and `content_link_chart_x_residual` (charts crossed with `attention_residual`, predictive route).

Tests: `tests/test_content_link_chart_20261003.py` (15 tests) covers:
- $\varphi_1$ against its series;
- the chart identities, and $\det(TO')>0$ in a case where the affine determinant is negative;
- equal initial content in every chart, both directly and through the model on both routes;
- gradcheck of the chart maps;
- short training runs on both routes;
- the post-hoc readers;
- config rejection of an unknown chart.

No GPU workload or training comparison has been run.

## Linear decoder learning rate

The linear readout (`output_proj_weight`, `output_proj_bias`) is already its own optimizer group at `m_decode_mu_lr`, falling back to `m_p_mu_lr` when None (`vfe3/train.py`, `_decode_learning_rates` and `build_optimizer`). The `build_optimizer` docstring said `m_p_mu_lr`; it now names `m_decode_mu_lr`. No new field was added: the linear readout and the untied prior-bank decode means never coexist, so one knob serves both.

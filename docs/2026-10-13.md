# October 13, 2026

## Zero-jitter Cholesky failures at K = 128

The live launcher (`gaussian_full`, kernel readout, `moment_matched`, `block_glk`, K = 128, four heads) stopped at step 231 with "projected covariance is not positive definite: zero-jitter Cholesky failed", at `fp32_matmul_precision = "high"` and again on the `"highest"` replay. A reproduction on seed 6 failed at step 190. The failing matrix is the belief covariance itself, read by the float64 source-root recovery (`relational_scoring._source_root`): its eigenvalues ran from 0.26 to 7.7e7, so the float32 rounding scale of about 9 exceeds the smallest eigenvalues and the stored matrix is indefinite. The factorization refuses it correctly; the defect is upstream.

Per-layer probes located the growth. Over the first 160 steps the mean leaving the attention update grew from about 12 to 4.5e3 in norm, while the prior means stayed of order 10. The block applies the E-step, then the block norm, then the MLP. The moment-matched covariance carries the routed mean spread `sum_j beta_ij n_ij n_ij^T`, of order the squared mean scale, and under `block_norm_covariance = "passthrough"` the norm rescales the mean but not the covariance, so the covariance keeps the large scale. The full family has no variance ceiling (`sigma_max` acts on the diagonal family only). `block_norm_covariance = "pushforward"` was tried on the same seed and failed at step 1 at both precisions. The cause of the mean growth itself was not identified; frame conditioning (`phi_frame_condition_max = None`) and the table M-step rates are the open candidates.

## Moment-matched covariance ceiling

`predictive_covariance_ceiling` (None by default, so the exact moment-matched path is unchanged; added to the `train_vfe3.py` dictionary at None) bounds the output of both moment-matched readouts by `c * Sigma_p` in the Loewner order, the counterpart of the prior-anchored floor, so every layer's output lies in `f Sigma_p <= Sigma^+ <= c Sigma_p` and its condition number is at most `(c / f) cond(Sigma_p)`. With `Sigma_p = L L^T` the clamp replaces the eigenvalues of `W = L^{-1} Sigma^+ L^{-T}` above `c` by `c`; the diagonal family takes the elementwise minimum, and the head-block family clamps each head block. Like the floor it involves no frame, so it is exactly invariant under a common reference change. A Cholesky certificate of `c Sigma_p - Sigma^+` selects the entries that are clamped (one host read per readout call), and those are whitened and clamped in float64. The backward is the Daleckii-Krein formula for `min(lambda, c)`, which stays finite on the eigenvalues the clamp makes equal. Clamped entries are counted in the new run-total counter and metrics.csv column `covariance_ceiling_elements`. The config refuses `c <= predictive_covariance_floor`, and the field is marked inactive wherever the floor is. Code is in `vfe3/model/covariance_ceiling.py` and `RelationalOperators.ceiling_term`.

`tests/test_covariance_ceiling_20261013.py` (14 cases, float64 CPU, both readouts, diagonal and full families) checks the identity when off, the band with a ceiling that binds on some tokens only, unchanged entries below the ceiling, idempotence, exact invariance under a reference change with cond(a) = 10, the backward against central finite differences including tied clamped eigenvalues, and the config refusal. A partial GPU run of the live launcher with `c = 100` passed both earlier failure steps without the error and was stopped at step 250. By then the ceiling had clamped about 1.2e5 entries, and the sampled largest eigenvalue was still 5e4. The ceiling is relative to the learned prior covariance, so the absolute bound moves with it.

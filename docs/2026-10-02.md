# Changes — October 2, 2026

## Chunk relational diagnostic eigensolver batches

`VFEModel._relational_diagnostics` now calls the existing `safe_eigvalsh` helper for stacked projected query/key covariance matrices. The helper chunks batches above 16,384 matrices, avoiding cuSOLVER’s small-matrix batched eigensolver call limit while preserving the eigenvalues.

Validation: model source parses and a tiny CPU comparison matches `torch.linalg.eigvalsh`. No CUDA reproduction or training was run.

## projected_gradient receiver laws and updates

Implemented the projected_gradient fixes derived in `Research/manuscripts/Attention/projected_gradient.tex` and the route-specific findings of `docs/audits/projected-gradient-pr30-32-audit-2026-10-01.md`. The PR #30 receiver remains selectable unchanged.

Three configuration fields were added. `relational_receiver_law` selects `"channel_likelihood"` (default) or `"ambient_alignment"`. The channel law scores the receiver's partial law $O_h^\top\xi_i$ against the sender's $d$-dimensional message $T_h\xi_j$ as an expected likelihood with channel noise $\nu I_d$, pooling $d\times d$ blocks and lifting them through the tied measurement $P_h=O_h^\top$. It counts the receiver entropy once ($c=\alpha$), its minimizer is the exact tempered Gaussian posterior, and directions no head observes keep the prior. The ambient law is the PR #30 energy, with $c=\alpha+\sum_h\lambda_h$ and zero-mean pinning of each head's unwritten coordinates. `relational_receiver_update` selects `"closed_form"` (default) or `"euler"`. The closed form applies the exact frozen-context natural-parameter update $J^+=(1-\rho)\Sigma^{-1}+\rho\Lambda/c$, $h^+=(1-\rho)\Sigma^{-1}\mu+\rho\eta/c$ with `relational_receiver_damping` $\rho\in(0,1]$. That is the exact Fisher flow for `gaussian_full`, and the restricted minimizer at $\rho=1$ for the diagonal family. `"euler"` is the PR #30 step. Serialized configs without these fields reload as `ambient_alignment` with `euler`, so saved PR #30 runs keep their receiver. Under `closed_form`, `inactive_fields` reports the E-step rates and trust radii as inert; under `euler` it reports the damping as inert.

Audit fixes on this route follow:

- **F4.** `n_e_steps=0` is rejected for projected_gradient.
- **F4, F13.** The episode captures (receiver cost after, repair count, displacement) are now written after the E-step loop in `e_step`. They exist for every iteration count, the repair window opens before the context build so routing-score recoveries are counted, and the displacement covers the whole episode.
- **F3.** New diagnostics `relational_receiver_mean_cost_delta`, `relational_receiver_covariance_cost_delta` and `relational_receiver_target_distance_ratio` (with `val_` columns) separate the mean and covariance parts of the frozen energy and measure the distance to $\mu^\star=\Lambda^{-1}\eta$.
- **F6.** Receiving-law factorizations use `cholesky_ex` with a counted float64 retry.
- **F7a.** `full_link_gradients` no longer rounds its force to a half-precision incoming mean.
- **F9.** The Q/K/V trace accepts the configured `n_e_steps` and attaches the receiver costs.
- **F14.** `RelationalOperators` accepts any finite real `receiver_noise`, matching config validation.
- **Artifacts.** Fields record the receiver law, update and damping.
- **Docs.** README documents the fields.

`lambda_alpha > 0` is required under `channel_likelihood`, because the prior divergence carries the only entropy term.

Files: `vfe3/gradients/full_link.py`, `vfe3/inference/e_step.py`, `vfe3/model/relational_operators.py`, `vfe3/model/model.py`, `vfe3/config.py` (fields, validation, inactive marks, loader default, preserving the existing WIP values), `vfe3/train.py`, `vfe3/run_artifacts.py`, `vfe3/viz/extract.py`, `tests/profiles.py` (historical receiver pinned for existing tests), the new `tests/test_projected_gradient_receiver.py`, and `README.md`. ## Configuration simplification after the projected-route retirement

After PR #32, only projected_predictive and projected_gradient remain. The projected envelope forces most inference and geometry selectors to one value, so the October 1 TODO list (`docs/audits/config-todos-2026-10-01.md`) was re-evaluated against current source. Plan, evidence and user decisions: `docs/audits/simplify-2026-10-02/PLAN.md` (local, ignored).

Removed config fields: `e_step_update` and `mm_damping` (mm_exact was already rejected by validation), `mu_trust_mode` (the Mahalanobis ball is now the only trust region), `divergence_family`, `renyi_order`, `include_attention_entropy`, `gradient_mode`, `e_step_gradient`, `oracle_unroll_grad`, `e_phi_lr`, `transport_mode`, `cross_couplings`, `e_step_mu_precond`, `full_cov_kl_backend`, the three congruence-precision toggles, `rope_full_gauge`, `m_decode_lr`, and the `_allow_legacy_dense_decode` InitVar. Also retired: the `gaussian_diagonal_exact` family (renamed to `gaussian_diagonal` on load), the `gaussian_frame_diagonal` family, the `canonical_content_gauge` and `canonical_content_projected` encoders, the dense decode modes (renamed to their `*_chunked` forms on load), and the glk, tied_block_glk, so_k, sp_n and sp gauge groups. `config_from_serialized` handles every retired key. It accepts a key carrying the forced value, or a value that was inert for that config, and raises a "retired" error where the old value would change numbers; `tests/test_config_retired_fields_20261002.py` covers these rules. No checkpoint parameter name changed.

Removed unreachable code: the mm_exact and hand-kernel gradient route (`gradients/kernels.py`, `oracle.py`, `pairwise_stats.py`), the transported full-Gaussian scorers (`families/full_gaussian_score.py`, `exact_congruence.py`, `frame_gaussian.py`), `geometry/closure.py`, `model/canonical_content.py`, the transported pair-energy and congruence stack in `geometry/transport.py`, `free_energy.py`, `e_step.py` and the family modules, the always-false `conditional_filtering_enabled` branches of `model.py` with the legacy extractors, the dead mm_exact diagnostics, and `estep_target_gap`. The gauge-equivariance report figure now tests the projected score: `extract.projected_gauge_equivariance` checks the routing score, beta, and the predictive readout or projected_gradient receiver force, update and energy under a random passive frame action. Its residuals are about 1e-13 at float64; `tests/test_projected_gauge_equivariance.py` covers it. Unrelated fix: post-hoc evaluation now captures the realized frame for `decode_coordinates='reference'`.

`gauge_group='so_n'` is reconnected to both projected routes. The irrep blocks must be `n_heads` equal blocks, one per head, because the operators, scorer and per-head priors read `group.irrep_dims`. Unequal irrep towers would need heads decoupled from irrep blocks, which is left open. so_n equivariance residuals are about 1.5e-13, and a finite-difference check on phi agrees to about 1e-10.

Verification: on seven tiny configs covering both routes, diagonal and full families, euler and closed_form, the canonical_frame MLP and a prior-bank decoder, loss, parameter names and parameter and gradient sums are identical to `f369760`. The final full CPU suite, run in 14 shards, recorded 2,946 cases: 2,934 passed, 12 skipped for environmental reasons, and 0 failures or errors. The pristine `f369760` baseline was 3,353 passed with 0 failures; the difference is deleted tests of deleted code.

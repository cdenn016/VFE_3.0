Comparison-space RoPE rotates each token's projected moments, a to R_i a and G to R_i G R_iᵀ, so the pair cost is KL(N(a_q, G_q) ‖ N(R_{j−i} a_k, R_{j−i} G_k R_{j−i}ᵀ)). The self pair is the only one compared without a relative rotation. For every other pair the key's covariance is rotated against the query's, and the trace term tr(G_k⁻¹ R G_q Rᵀ) grows with the anisotropy of the comparison covariances (cond(G_k) = 590 in L1 h0), while the mean term grows with |a|² through the rotated mean mismatch. Dot-product RoPE has no covariance term and no distance penalty for rotation, so this is specific to a Gaussian-KL routing cost. The penalty feeds back: as training grows anisotropy and mean norms, every non-self cost rises, routing concentrates on self, and once the gaps pass `kl_max` the capped keys stop receiving routing gradient. In frame-space RoPE the rotation acts before the learned maps S, which can absorb or project out the rotating planes, and ALiBi and learned positional frames rotate nothing. The 32.65 run (also comparison RoPE) had a layer-1 Mahalanobis blowup with median query-mean norm 4016, which is consistent with the same mechanism. Candidate fixes, none implemented: make the comparison covariance commute with the rotations (its projection onto the commutant of the RoPE torus keeps one isotropic variance per rotation plane and drops cross-plane covariance), which makes the trace and log-determinant terms position-free and leaves only the mean term relative; or rotate the means only, which removes the covariance penalty but leaves the cost dependent on absolute position through G_k⁻¹; or use p-RoPE (`rope_fraction` below 1) to limit the rotating planes.

## Addendum: wording of punch-list item 4

Frames are gauge frames by construction. The finding is that, because they are pure gauge, wherever every consumer is covariant they are removable by g_i = U_i⁻¹ (the manuscript's dressing-field remark at :893), so a learned-frame model there equals a frame-free model with a per-type (ξ_v, C_v) table (`block_cholesky`). Any measured frame benefit must therefore come through the non-covariant parts of the stack (local LayerNorm and MLP, the local decoder, and the diagonal family's moment projection, which is not closed under block-GL congruence) or through that implied per-type covariance storage. The missing control is identity frames with the `block_cholesky` table on a reference-coordinate base.

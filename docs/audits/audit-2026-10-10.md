# Deep audit, October 10, 2026

## Scope

The audit covered the whole VFE_3.0 repository (414 tracked Python files, 108 of them in `vfe3/`) at `main` = `7bfdf27` (the merge of the round-2 remediation, PR #88), together with the user's uncommitted work in `train_vfe3.py`, `vfe3/config.py`, `ablation.py`, `train_baseline.py`, `paper_arms.py`, `multiseed_analysis.py`, `zzzzz.py`, `vfe3/viz/figure_worker.py`, three test files and several docs. The active configuration was the `train_vfe3.py` launcher dictionary: K=20 with two heads, four layers, `projected_predictive` with the kernel readout, `gaussian_diagonal`, the `diagonal_chunked` decoder, moment-matched predictive covariance (floor 1e-2), the exponential content chart, Gaussian-KL routing with `routing_kl_max_reference="row_min"` and `kl_max = 8K`, comparison-space RoPE with `rope_comparison_covariance="full"` under the `causal_alibi` prior, no attention residual, the coordinate block MLP with a shared LayerNorm in local coordinates and passthrough covariance, block_glk per-head frames updated by AdamW, bf16 autocast and `fp32_matmul_precision="high"`. The working tree also flips two `VFE3Config` defaults, `rope_space` to `"comparison"` (pinned in `_HISTORICAL_DEFAULT_PINS`) and `routing_kl_max_reference` to `"row_min"` (unpinned; the user declined that pin after round 2). The planned paper base (`paper_arms.py`, `k128_alibi` = `36.41_wikitext-103_K128_alibi-exp-chunk`: K=128, four heads of 32, ALiBi, `pos_rotation="none"`, `row_min`) was evaluated where it matters. The GPU was running the user's training job (96 percent utilization), so every investigator worked read-only and CPU-only, used tiny models or read saved checkpoints, deleted its scratch files, and cited executable source. No source was edited by the audit.

## Investigators dispatched

Wave 1 ran all nine research-domain experts (gauge-theorist, geometer, info-geometer, variational, transformer-ML, numerical analyst, ML engineer, implementation engineer, philosophy of science), because the request covered the whole codebase and theory, and one general-purpose sweep of the core hot path (`vfe3/model`, `vfe3/gradients`, `vfe3/relational_scoring.py`, `vfe3/geometry`, `vfe3/families`, `vfe3/inference`, `vfe3/numerics.py`). Wave 2 ran two further general-purpose sweeps, launched as wave-1 agents finished so that concurrent CPU probes would not starve the training job: training, configuration, data, artifacts and the entrypoints; and the analysis layers (`vfe3/viz`, `vfe3/semantics`, `vfe3/posthoc`, `vfe3/paper` and their drivers). Each investigator received the active configuration, the project invariants and the round-1 and round-2 punch lists, with instructions not to re-report remediated items and to tag reopened ones. Because the user asked for improvements as well as defects, each investigator could return concrete improvement proposals, marked as such below. All agents ran on the session model (Opus 5.5). The JEV advisory screen was not run.

## Findings by investigator

### Philosophy of science

The paper-arms program has already run the TIER_A "matched total parameters" transformer on the 36.41 base protocol (same data hashes, the same 280,541 test targets, seeds (6, 3) and (23, 23)), and it reverses the README's headline comparison (high; `README.md:660, 684-695`, `vfe3_runs/paper_arms/k128_alibi/base_protocol/tf_matched_total/`). At 231,271,249 total parameters, of which 112.2M process context, it reaches test CE 3.3620 and 3.3681 against the VFE's 3.5922 (`test_best_ce` of 36.41), a paired difference of 0.230 nats in the transformer's favor, in 7,550 s against 33,428 s of wall time, with an untuned learning rate and 120k steps against 150k, both of which favor the VFE. The coordinator read the five `summary.json` files and confirmed these values. The README's "parameter-matched" and "removes the parameter confound" describe a comparison matched at the VFE's allocation (1.3M context parameters and a 206M per-type table), and the total-matched arm is the test of the rival explanation that the gap comes from forcing the comparator into that allocation. Four medium items follow. The registered 0.03-nat margin is smaller than the comparison's dependence on which held-out split is scored, because `paired_difference` (`vfe3/paper/tables.py:384-417`) takes its SE over at most three seed pairs on one test split and so contains no test-sample component: the VFE-minus-transformer difference for the (6, 3) pair moves by 0.073 to 0.080 nats between validation and test, and multi-epoch K=128 VFE runs show val-to-test CE shifts of 0.026 to 0.086 against 0.006 to 0.019 for the transformers, while the paired article-level bootstrap in `vfe3/posthoc/evaluate.py` that would capture this is called nowhere in `vfe3/paper` (the margin itself is the user's registered decision; the missing variance component is the finding). The frame-free head-block arms added after round-2 item 4 resolve against the base (232,899,840 and 126,757,056 parameters) but sit in no tier and feed no decision clause (`paper_arms.py:355-363, 493-500`; `tables.py:512-529`), so a full TIER_A plus TIER_B program still cannot overturn the rival "per-type covariance storage". Run folders are still named by test perplexity (round-1 #30), and the user's WIP experiment note `docs/experiments/2026-10-09_prototype-K128-workvar-collapse.md:5-6` already reads two of those names as validation perplexities (52.03 is test, its validation is 48.20). As an improvement, a dated retraction condition for the comparative claim should be registered now, together with the claim the data do support (at a fixed 1.3M context budget the construction turns a roughly 230M per-type table into 0.657 nats over a transformer without one and 0.469 nats over one with an equal read-only table). Low items: the edge-link null claims a resolution of 0.01 nats where the repository's own `flag_noise_dominated` band gives 0.023 (`README.md:735`); README lines 494-495 describe a detached row minimum and a hard temperature clamp that no toggle now produces; the novelty sentence at `README.md:24-25` omits earlier attention scored by divergences between per-token Gaussians (Fan et al. 2022, arXiv:2201.06035; Erick et al. 2023, arXiv:2311.18645); and the prototype-collapse note's mechanism sentence rests on timing alone because one learning rate moves ν, λ and log π together.

### ML engineer

On the paper base, 32 test targets that occur at most about four times in training carry 20,750 nats, 0.074 nats per token of the headline test CE (high; `vfe3/train.py:291-296`, `vfe3/model/prior_bank.py:1473, 1560`, `vfe3/run_artifacts.py:3982-4003`). A CPU fp32 replay of `final_model.pt` over every fourth sliding window (matching the model CE to 5e-6) found single tokens costing 1,636 to 1,827 nats and window CEs up to 81 nats per token, against a validation maximum of 8.6. Without those 32 targets the test CE is 3.5213 (perplexity 33.82 instead of 36.41) and the val-to-test gap falls from 0.086 to 0.023 nats. The mechanism sits in the untied decode table: all 2,319 zero-count types' `decode_mu_embed` rows moved coherently to one point (norm 44.5, mean pairwise cosine 0.996, against norm 4.3 for frequent rows), which makes the unbounded quadratic logit of `diagonal_chunked` reach a median gap of 1,842 nats, against 49 in the 32.65 run. How those rows kept moving past the fp32 softmax-underflow gap was not isolated. The finding bears directly on the 0.03-nat decision margin, since arms that perturb the decoder or its training can move this tail. Medium items: the `grow_K_mup` route scales the input table, which μP holds fixed, and leaves the hidden matrices at fixed rates, and its guard admits `projected_gradient` with `closed_form`, under which `e_q_mu_lr` is inert (`scaling.py:460-482, 1386`; round-2 #18 incomplete); `val_attn_collapsed_heads` counts a head as collapsed if any single row of the first validation window has normalized entropy below 0.5, so it saturates (6 of 8 heads in 106.80 against 2 by per-head mean; `vfe3/train.py:2915-2919`, `vfe3/metrics.py:897-906`). Improvements: the `pullback_group` step is 85 times smaller than AdamW's at the same `m_phi_lr` because it scales with the row's token count (`vfe3/gauge_optim.py:466`), and the fixed-sample generalization gap could be split into served and unserved text using the replayable data permutation.

### Transformer-ML

The VFE validator admits the non-causal priors `uniform`, `alibi` and `windowed` for next-token training and scoring without refusal (medium; `vfe3/config.py:1265`): tiny models built with each moved earlier-position logits by up to 2.6 when later tokens changed, while the baseline refuses anything outside `causal` and `causal_alibi` (`vfe3/baseline.py:293-295`), and `inactive_fields` labels `windowed` at the default window "identical to plain causal" although it equals the uniform prior. `beta_channel_decomposition` attributes RoPE position and the self-copy path to "content" (medium; `vfe3/run_artifacts.py:4340-4442`): on the 96.20 checkpoint, removing the comparison rotation alone raises CE by 2.140 of the 2.265 nats the record calls content, and the saved 32.65 record reports a positional channel of 0.0 for a model whose only position signal is the rotated cost. Low items: the context-sensitivity record is measured at layer 0's attention output rather than the final belief (1.504 recorded against 1.042 at the final belief), and the residual-ratio panels are titled ‖m_i‖/‖μ_i‖ while under `attention_residual=False` they plot a displacement (`vfe3/model/stack.py:144`, `vfe3/viz/figures.py:2585, 3707`); `generate_text.stream_ce` scores disjoint blocks with a mean context near 64 tokens and reports 80.7 perplexity where the stride-32 protocol gives 75.4 on the same tokens (`generate_text.py:283-302`). Improvement: the stack is causal and prefix-consistent to 1.1e-5, so an exact per-layer incremental cache for `generate` is available (`vfe3/model/model.py:1458-1461`).

### Variational

The `channel_likelihood` receiver of `projected_gradient` measures with O'ᵀ, the adjoint of the receiving map, rather than its inverse (medium; `vfe3/gradients/full_link.py:446`). In fp64 with equal channel precisions its minimizer is 3.73 away from the kernel mean (1.6e-15 with the dual measurement), and under the reparametrization (U h, T h, S h, h⁻¹O'), which leaves routing and the kernel mean invariant to 1e-13, the minimizer moves 12.7 at α→0 and 0.56 at the defaults; trained O' is far from orthogonal (relative ‖O'ᵀ − O'⁻¹‖ of 0.80 to 0.94 on 96.20, cond up to 3.3e16 at K=128). This route is not active, but `channel_likelihood` is its default law. The canonical free energy is still reachable under no toggle (round-2 #22, open; `vfe3/model/model.py:1819`), although its ingredients are captured and the envelope identity holds to 7.1e-15. The learned temperature still divides after a cap set in KL units, so at the temperature ceiling at K=20 the capped keys carry 0.911 of a row's mass with zero gradient (low; round-2 #14 in part; `vfe3/gradients/relational.py:273`). The block LayerNorm rescales the mean but never the covariance (medium; `vfe3/model/block.py:147`): the median LayerNorm scale per layer on 96.20 is 0.67, 1.80, 1.28 and 0.96, and handing the next layer diag(J_LN Σ J_LNᵀ) instead moved routing by mean total variation 0.18 to 0.48, so the covariance the router reads is load-bearing and in pre-norm units (this coincides with the geometer's item below). Improvements: the minimal-agent and prototype mean field run a single coordinate-ascent step, and on the 52.03 run the labels used differ from the labels recomputed at the posterior by total variation 0.14 to 0.84 per layer (`vfe3/model/minimal_agent.py:769`); and the template prior, template means and channel noise train on CE alone with no EM restoring term (`vfe3/model/model.py:1299`), where 52.03 ends with prior entropies down to 2e-78 nats and one effective template per layer. The moment-matched covariance matches brute-force enumeration to 5e-16, and the posterior and CAVI identities hold.

### Geometer

The diagonal family has no way to push its covariance through the block LayerNorm or MLP, and the config gives a false reason for refusing `delta_full` there (medium, improvement; `vfe3/config.py:1617-1623`): the stated reason, that JΣJᵀ "cannot be represented by a diagonal family", is contradicted by the code's own diagonal M-projection of a dense covariance in the attention update (`vfe3/model/relational_operators.py:994, 1168`). Trace ratios tr diag(JΣJᵀ)/tr Σ on 96.20 are 8.56, 0.358, 1.44 and 1.51 per layer, and on the 32.65 K=128 run down to 0.0163, so passthrough is off the first-order pushforward by up to a factor of 60; the diagonal projection (J∘J)σ + f d²σ needs no K×K factorization. Under `delta_full` with a LayerNorm, J_LN·1 = 0 leaves one direction per token whose variance is set only by the floor f, so full-family KLs scale as 1/f (medium; `vfe3/model/block_mlp.py:164-194, 354-368`): adjacent-token KLs are about 1e4 nats at f = 1e-4 and about 100 at f = 1e-2, and the next layer's router caps up to 16.6 percent of causal gaps at f = 1e-4. Low items: the reference-coordinate block has no covariance pushforward under any toggle (an improvement that would give the covariant stack a propagated Σ); `pullback_group` is still refused at d=32 even under `group_log`, which needs no structure constants (round-1 #41, round-2 #38); and the retraction registry, BCH stack, `retract_phi` and `safe_spd_inverse` have no production caller (round-1 #42). The SPD retraction matches the AIRM exponential to 2.4e-15, the frame Fisher route recovers the ambient gradient to 1.3e-15, and trained frames have condition numbers at most 7.5.

### Info-geometer

As an improvement, no update on the live route uses a Gaussian Fisher metric, and preconditioning the tables before AdamW cannot help because Adam is invariant to a constant diagonal rescaling of the gradient; an opt-in natural-gradient step for the mean and log-variance tables, (σ²∂μ, 2∂ℓ) with a per-row trust radius, is an O(VK) elementwise update (medium; `vfe3/train.py:284-296, 449`). On the 96.20 checkpoint `sigma_log_embed` still has mean 1.6104 against its initial log 5 = 1.6094 and standard deviation 0.082, which connects to round-1's open item that the covariance table is effectively untrained. Low items: the config comment for `tie_comparison_maps` claims D_ij = KL(q_i‖(Ω_ij)_*q_j), while the executed cost is a per-head rank-d marginal KL bounded above by it (4.970 against a per-head sum of 1.805 in an fp64 probe; `vfe3/config.py:394-395`); and `FullGaussian.natural()` adds a 1e-6 ridge that `expected_statistic()` does not, biasing the generic log-partition oracle by 9.5e-3 relative at a smallest eigenvalue of 1e-4 (`vfe3/families/gaussian.py:639`). Every closed-form divergence matched fp64, mpmath or quadrature references, the router computes KL(q_i‖k_j) in the stated direction, decode CE matches an independent fp64 computation to 4.6e-7 (K=20) and 2.6e-6 (K=128), and the live pair-KL GEMM error on 36.41 is at most 2.2e-4 nats, at most 9e-5 near the row minimum (relevant to round-2 #7).

### Gauge-theorist

The frame-covariant path exists, is reachable, and is broader than the README states: in fp64 with beliefs and frames moved per token, comparison-space RoPE (both covariance options), `gaussian_block`, every group (block_so, block_co, block_glmc, so_n tied and untied, shared tying), and the `gauge_gate` and `canonical_frame` MLPs with norm none are covariant to 1e-13 or better, while frame-space RoPE is refused by the validator rather than merely non-covariant (low; `README.md:31-35`, and line 41 says "with a residual connection" while the launcher has none). The decoder casts float64 beliefs to float32 (`vfe3/model/model.py:890-895`, also found by two other investigators below). `_pure_path_report` marks `canonical_frame` non-pure although it is the same dressing construction as reference coordinates and passes the passive-action check to 2.5e-13 (low; `vfe3/run_artifacts.py:5042`). so_n irrep generators are stored in float32, so a float64 model carries rounded irreps and CG coupling is equivariant only to about 1e-8 (low; `vfe3/geometry/groups.py:560, 635`). Improvement: block_so, block_co, block_glmc, shared-tied block_glk and equal-dimension so_n produce exactly block-diagonal frames but take the dense transport route, about 9.5K³ against 2K³ multiply-adds per token plus one host-synchronizing off-block check per scoring call, and `gaussian_block` is refused for them although `check_admissible` accepts it (medium; `vfe3/geometry/groups.py:666-686`, `vfe3/config.py:1123-1125`).

### Numerical analyst

The decoder and fused-CE seams call `.float()` unconditionally, so a `.double()` model decodes from fp32-rounded beliefs (medium; `vfe3/model/model.py:891-893, 1229, 1234, 1240, 1245, 1282, 1288`; `vfe3/model/prior_bank.py:2777`). A dispatch-mode probe found this to be the only fp64→fp32 cast in the whole forward. Whole-model central-difference gradient checks in fp64 have relative residuals of 1.2e-3 to 4.4e-3 at h = 1e-6 as shipped and 3.2e-10 to 5.1e-10 with the cast replaced by `promote_types(x.dtype, float32)`; the core sweep found a worst relative error of 1.0 on one parameter and the gauge-theorist a 7.8e-5 floor on fp64 covariance checks. The cast is value-identical in fp32 and harmless to training; what it breaks is every fp64 reference built with `model.double()`. The IEEE pins cover only the forward pass of the frame-sensitive kernels (round-2 #32): under emulated TF32 (operands rounded to a 10-bit mantissa on CPU, a lower bound, not a GPU measurement) parameter-gradient relative errors at K=128 reach 5.5e-2 for layer-0 O and 3.5e-2 for `sigma_log_embed`, against at most 7e-4 at IEEE fp32, with a CE shift of 9.1e-5 (low). The factor-route pair-KL tile, used only on the recovery path, is the one scorer tile without an IEEE pin (low; `vfe3/relational_scoring.py:868-872, 1040-1061`). Improvements: the φ₁ chart's backward builds a 4K×4K fp64 matrix exponential, and the product form e^{OT}T⁻¹ is 3.6 times cheaper on the 36.41 tables with values equal to 1.7e-13; and AdamW's ε = 1e-8 exceeds √v̂ for 78 percent of touched entries of `mu_embed` and `sigma_log_embed`, cutting the σ-table step by about 35 percent (`vfe3/train.py:449`). End to end, IEEE fp32 matches true fp64 to 5.7e-6 in CE at K=128, bf16 touches only the MLP, and `kl_max` under `row_min` is numerically inert.

### Implementation engineer

`inactive_fields` misses two optimizer-only knobs (medium; `vfe3/config.py:1859-2186`): `weight_decay` reaches only the block-MLP and CG groups, and `phi_weight_decay` is overridden by the manifold policies' `weight_decay: 0.0` metadata, so three-step bitwise probes with `use_block_mlp=False`, `pullback_group` or `fisher_group` change nothing, the ablation dead-sweep guard does not see it, and the scheduled `weight_decay` and `phi_weight_decay` sweeps would run identical arms on such baselines. The unpinned `routing_kl_max_reference` flip reloads only 32.65 and 34.48 under `row_min` (22 configs reloaded; the current 36.41 base records the field, so the paper reuse is correct), and since the user declined the pin, the suggestion is to annotate or remove the two affected BASES entries (low; round-2 #1). `paper_arms` reports "total parameters differ from the base record" on every VFE base because it compares a count that excludes the frozen `decode_log_scale` with one that includes it, so the check cannot detect real drift (low; `paper_arms.py:1004-1008`). The round-2 #37 WIP launcher defects remain (`train_baseline.py:81, 84` points at a renamed directory; two ablation sweeps cannot construct), and three analysis entrypoints accept positional command-line arguments against the click-to-run policy (low; `posthoc_eval.py:94-96`, `analyze_semantics.py:71-73`, `multiseed_semantics.py:49-54`). Improvements: a checked-in snapshot of dataclass defaults with a test that fails on any unpinned, unwaived default change (an AST walk over 54 commits found 31 default changes, of which only `routing_kl_max_reference` is unpinned among post-57d9ea0 fields; medium), and defaulting `train()`'s loop knobs from cfg while deleting the deprecated `run_training`, which validates on the training split (low). Every inert-marked field is bitwise inert on four routes, every SWEEP_ORDER arm constructs, and the paper-arms dry run resolves every arm.

### General sweep: core hot path

Beyond the fp64 decode cast (counted with the numerical analyst's item), the tie still factors the same comparison covariance twice (round-2 #31, remediation incomplete; `vfe3/relational_scoring.py:769-771`; low, inactive). Three performance improvements were measured on CPU at the paper-base shape. The key-side Cholesky backward costs two batched triangular solves per layer, 9.3 percent of a K=128 step, while a closed-form backward gG = −P sym(g_P) P + g_ld P took 18.8 ms against 231.8 ms in a microbenchmark with gradients equal to 3.7e-7 (medium; `vfe3/relational_scoring.py:771, 406, 777-778`). Folding Σ^{1/2} into the frame blocks for the diagonal family cuts the router projection's forward and backward by 31 percent with all outputs and gradients equal to 6e-7 (medium; `vfe3/relational_scoring.py:313-339`). The φ₁ chart costs five times the product form (low to medium; round-1 #21; the same as the numerical analyst's item). Two host syncs per step from `bounded_variance_from_log` decide only whether to print a warning, and the chart-radius check only raises, so all three could ride the deferred host-check queue (low; `vfe3/numerics.py:62`, `vfe3/geometry/transport.py:1121`). Whole-model fp64 finite-difference checks agree with autograd to 1.5e-3 under eleven paper-arm toggle sets, and the code added by `e9e0bbc` behaves as documented.

### General sweep: training, configuration, data and entrypoints

A failed log-step diagnostic aborts training at the next `metrics.csv` write (medium; `vfe3/train.py:3873-3881, 4220, 4279`, `vfe3/run_artifacts.py:2025-2030`): the fallback row carries 9 keys, `log_metrics` refuses a changed field set, and an injected failure produced "TRAINING ABORTED ... metrics row field set changed"; the diagnostic forward runs outside the replay ladder at TF32, where the 32.65 run needed 266 replays. A transient `torch.save` failure on `best_model.pt` likewise aborts training, while the same failure on a checkpoint is caught (medium; `vfe3/train.py:4094`, `vfe3/run_artifacts.py:2071-2083`; an injected "ios_base::badbit set" aborted at step 2 of 6). Low items: eight CUDA-only eigensolver tests are skipped in the CPU lane and not selected by `check_gpu_tests.py`, and no GPU-lane test runs a training step, resume or evaluation on the device, while the empty slow lane leaves its infrastructure dead (`tests/test_eigvalsh_batch_ceiling_20260807.py`, `check_gpu_tests.py:38`); and a resumed run's `summary.json` wall time and `resources.json` device-hours cover only the continuation (code-traced, related to round-2 #35). Improvements: the self-loop spectral radius runs 4,096 host fp64 eigensolves of 128×128 matrices per log row at K=128, a median `log_diagnostics_ms` of 22 s against a 265 ms step and 66 percent of the 36.41 run's non-training wall time, while the maximum is attained at position 0 and equals ρ(W_full) on the trained checkpoint (medium; `vfe3/metrics.py:1840-1853`); eval-cadence publication blocks for 6 to 21 s on a figure-worker subprocess and a 0.93 GB save, and each checkpoint embeds a duplicate of `best_model.pt` (3.73 GB per checkpoint); and the launch pipeline exists twice (`train_vfe3._run_once` and `vfe3/experiment.run_vfe`), with three copies of the directory helpers, two dead and misnamed aliases, and duplicate quantile helpers. Resume across epoch boundaries under `per_epoch_random` is bitwise exact, and loop overhead in 96.20 is 1.7 percent.

### General sweep: analysis, visualization, semantics and posthoc

Post-hoc frame interventions (rows 7, 9 and 11) report measured zero effects with zero-width intervals on runs whose frames are inactive (`gauge_transport="off"`), and `gap_applicability` would label a frame-free covariance gap as a frame quantity (medium; `vfe3/posthoc/interventions.py:234, 532-548, 650`, `vfe3/posthoc/covariance.py:79-87`); four paper arms set `gauge_transport="off"`. `aggregate_per_layer` in `multiseed_analysis.py` returns nothing whenever any column holds a NaN or text cell, which every current `metrics_per_layer.csv` does, so the per-layer channel is always withheld with a misleading reason, and five of its seven listed metrics are all-NaN on projected routes (medium; `multiseed_analysis.py:844-852`, a user-WIP file whose defect is in committed code). Under `block_cholesky` the Σ-section statistics use only the diagonal without saying so (round-2 #33 incomplete; `vfe3/semantics/run.py:252, 265-281`): on 118.94 the reported log det overstates the true one by a median 1.171 nats and diagonal KLs differ from block KLs by a median 20.4 percent (medium). Low items: single-seed scaling points receive a vanishing weight yet count toward the size count that unlocks R², the interval and lack-of-fit (`vfe3/viz/figures.py:4357-4371`); and the WikiText test segmentation finds 62 articles instead of 60 by matching two equation lines as headings (`vfe3/posthoc/streams.py:54`). Improvements: posthoc evaluates `best_model.pt` again when it is bitwise identical to `final_model.pt`, which holds for 13 of 16 runs on disk (medium); the per-head cap and holonomy CSVs are written but never plotted, although layer 1 head 0 of 96.20 caps 41 percent of its entries; and four figure builders and one duplicated helper have no production caller. Readers ran without crashes on diagonal, `gaussian_block`, `block_cholesky`, comparison-RoPE, `row_min` and ALiBi checkpoints, and the round-2 #5, #23, #25 and #44 remediations are verified.

## Addendum: exact cost of the rare-target tail (item 2)

The coordinator re-scored, in IEEE fp32 on the CPU, the 135 stride-32 test windows that score every target whose type occurs at most 30 times in training, and combined their exact NLLs with the recorded all-target test CE. For 36.41, the 10 targets whose type never occurs in training carry 16,979 nats (about 1,760 each, 0.0605 nats/token) and the 32 targets with count at most 3 carry 20,750 nats (0.0740 nats/token). Dropping those 32 gives test perplexity 33.83; pricing them at their add-one unigram cost instead, the counterfactual of a decoder that backs off, gives 33.88. For 32.65 (comparison RoPE, six layers, `sigma_init=15`) the same 32 targets cost 719 nats in total (at most 44 each), and the corresponding perplexities are 32.58 and 32.63. In 36.41 the decode log-variances never left their initialization (mean log σ² 1.618 against log 5 = 1.609), so the logit gap is about |μ_q − μ_v|²/(2σ²τ) = |Δμ|² at σ² = 5 and τ = 0.1. The zero-count decode rows were still drifting at the end of training (median norm 42.1 at step 90k, 44.5 at 120k, moving along their mean direction with cosine 0.98) under Adam second moments near 1e-10 and no weight decay on the decode means.

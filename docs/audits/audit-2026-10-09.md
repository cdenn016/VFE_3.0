## Findings by investigator

### Cross-cutting: layer 1 of the 32.65 run does no routing

Six investigators (transformer-ml, ml-engineer, info-geometer, variational, numerical-analyst, core general sweep) found this independently, with consistent evidence. The run predates `routing_kl_max_reference`, so it used the library default `"absolute"` (`vfe3/config.py:630`), under which `safe_kl_clamp` (`vfe3/gradients/relational.py:193`, `vfe3/families/base.py:69`) clamps every routing cost to `kl_max = 8K = 1024` with zero derivative above it. In `diagnostics/cap_activation.csv`, layer 1 is uncapped at step 10k, partially capped at 20k and 30k (head 1 fully by 30k), and `capped == entries == 66040` for all four heads at every evaluation from 40k to 120k except a brief 0.98 at 60k. The layer's attention entropy is 3.8782, exactly the uniform causal value, and `attention_stats.csv` reports `entropy_norm = 1.0` and `prior_overlap = 1.0`. A backward pass on `final_model.pt` gives exactly zero gradient on layer 1's `raw_score_q` and `raw_score_k`, against 2e-3 to 0.23 elsewhere. A replay with the clamp removed shows layer-1 raw costs with median near 1.1e4 to 3e4 nats, almost entirely the Mahalanobis term (median query-mean norm 4016 in layer 1 against 80 to 608 elsewhere); the score maps moved 10.7x and 16.7x their initial norms before freezing. Layer 3 head 0 is climbing the same path (capped fraction 0.163, 0.192, 0.251 over 100k to 120k). The headline `metrics.csv` column `relational_score_klmax_frac` read 0.0 throughout because it reduces only the last layer (`vfe3/model/model.py:1659,1888`). Because the base record lacks the field, every VFE arm `paper_arms.py` resolves on this base inherits `"absolute"`, while the live `train_vfe3.py:313` uses `"row_min"`.

The investigators disagree on how far `row_min` repairs this checkpoint, and the two results are compatible. The numerical analyst measured nonzero layer-1 score gradients under `row_min` (4.41e-3 and 2.75e-2, CE 3.2466 against 3.2463), and a log1p soft cap probe also restored them. The info-geometer and variational investigators found that 97.4% of row-minimum gaps on this checkpoint exceed 1024, so `row_min` routing in layer 1 is close to a hard argmin (normalized entropy about 0). Gradient flows through the few near-minimum sources while routing is near one-hot. The manuscript sentence that the bound binds "on none later" (`GL(K)_attention_v2.tex:1146`) holds for the 34.48 run (at most 2 capped entries) but not for 32.65, and the supplement's identification with the unmodified KL "requires an inactive bound" (`GL(K)_supplementary_v2.tex:1109`).

### Gauge-theorist

The gauge-pure path exists, is reachable and is exactly covariant. In an fp64 CPU check (K=8, H=2, d=4, two layers, comparison-space RoPE, moment-matched covariance, exponential chart), `gaussian_full` with `block_coordinates='reference'` and `decode_coordinates='reference'` gave Δlog p = 6.8e-14 under random per-token GL(4)² elements while the update moved μ by 5.3. The diagonal family is covariant under monomial elements only (7.5e-13), as `groups.py:323` declares. The dense Ω, cocycle, RoPE fold, irrep homomorphism, CG intertwiner and pullback-per-block paths hold under their contracts.

On the full-family reference arm the frame table telescopes (medium). Every consumer reads U only through ξ = U⁻¹μ and C = U⁻¹ΣU⁻ᵀ (`vfe3/model/block.py:161-226`, `relational_operators.py:505-546`, `gradients/relational.py:14-125`). Replacing each head block by U' = D^{1/2}Qᵀ C^{-1/2}, a non-passive change with ‖U'−U‖ = 3.9, left the logits unchanged to 7.7e-14, so d(d+1)/2 of the d² chart coordinates per head and type are redundant. On the diagonal family the same change moves Δlog p by 0.166, so there U acts as a learned per-type mean-field basis. The manuscript's per-layer "multiplicative token-conditioned map" (`GL(K)_attention_v2.tex:1196`) reduces on this arm to a one-time (ξ_v, C_v) reparameterization, and a frame-free (ξ_v, Cholesky C_v) table would be an exact, cheaper equivalent. The moment-matched floor f·(tr C/K)·I (`relational_operators.py:986-988`, `:1075`) breaks the right-GL redundancy at O(f) (Δlog p = 2.1e-5 at f = 1e-4, 4e-13 at f = 1e-12 or for orthogonal k), while `vfe3/semantics/frames.py:8-12,1724-1729` quotients by the full GL action (low); a right-covariant floor f·C_i or reporting O(d)^H as the exact right group would fix it. `require_basis_closed` (`lie_ops.py:195-231`) skips large bases by size, so block_glk at K=128 is never checked, although closure of a direct sum reduces to one block's local generators (low).

### Geometer

The live geometric kernels check out (exponential chart 2.3e-14, logm Fréchet derivative 7e-9 against finite differences, spectral backward 2e-9 to 3.5e-9, `fisher_type_solve` residual at most 4e-15, `pooled_pos_operator` 1.5e-14), and the pure natural-gradient frame path `fisher_group` with `group_log` is reachable.

A `gaussian_block` family storing one d×d SPD block per head would be exact and cheap for block_glk (medium). Block-diagonal SPD matrices are the smallest family closed under the block group's congruence; diagonal ones are closed only under monomial elements. The blockwise moment-matched route already forms `C_att` per head (`relational_operators.py:971`) and then keeps only its diagonal (`:989`, `gaussian.py:240`). In an executed check the next-layer reference covariance changed by 49.73 (against a norm of 16.57) under U→gU with the diagonal projection and by 1.09e-14 with the block projection, which would remove the "fifth frame channel" the manuscript names (`GL(K)_attention_v2.tex:948,1642`). The `diagonal_chunked` decoder serves a block family exactly, at K·d = 4096 floats per token against 128 (diagonal) and 16384 (full). The low findings are that `pullback_group` cannot be enabled at d=32 even with `group_log` (24576 MiB of structure constants against a 512 MiB budget, `gauge_optim.py:654-665`, `config.py:2072`), although off-strip rows could use the spectral Ψ inverse at `phi_preconditioner.py:1250`; that the retraction registry, `retract_phi`, the BCH stack (`compose_bch`, `retract_glk`, `retract_son`, `clamp_phi_trace` in `lie_ops.py`) and `safe_spd_inverse` (`numerics.py:375`) have no production callers; that `retraction.py:783` cites a supplement location that no longer contains the retraction; and that `GL(K)_attention_v2.tex:149` says attention updates the covariance "by congruence" where the evaluated update is moment-matched.

### Info-geometer

The divergence closed forms are correct (diagonal and full Gaussian KL and Rényi, generic Rényi from A(θ), Laplace, Bhattacharyya, the projected router KL against a direct full KL at 1.8e-15, the Gaussian natural gradient, and the frame Fisher pullback at 18.788 against 18.787). Beyond the layer-1 finding, no router parameter can set routing sharpness (medium): the KL is invariant under (S_Q, S_K)→(AS_Q, AS_K), as the manuscript states at `:508`, and τ = κ_β√d is a fixed configuration value (`free_energy.py:84-85`, `config.py:339`, `relational_scoring.py:784-785`). Layer 1's Mahalanobis term is about 400 times its neighbors' at similar map norms, and nothing could cool it; an opt-in learnable per-layer, per-head log κ_β (initialized at log κ) would restore a learnable temperature without changing the KL. The pair-KL tile runs under TF32 while the moments it consumes are pinned to IEEE fp32 (medium; `relational_scoring.py:764-785`): at cond(G) = 1e3 the maximum KL error is 1.0e-2 under IEEE fp32 and 2.3e-1 under TF32, and the maximum β error is 1.1e-4 against 4.9e-3. Query and key maps can never be tied on the projected_predictive route (medium; `relational_operators.py:433-436`, the retired `"transported"` mode refused at `config.py:690-694`), so the self-pair cost D_ii is nonzero (checkpoint medians 80, 48926, 50, 47, 33 and 32 nats by layer) and the manuscript's flat-alignment case and data-processing bound (`:508`, `:1580`) are untestable; an opt-in S_K := S_Q toggle would make them testable. The divergence-family registry and Rényi order are pinned (`config.py:2151-2152`), so Hellinger, Bhattacharyya, Jeffreys and α ≠ 1 are reachable only from tests while the docstrings (`base.py:379,438`) describe them as configuration values, and an α > 1 non-PD blend silently returns `kl_max` (low).

### Variational

The free-energy algebra is correct: the Gibbs/envelope identity, the exact detach of the stationary α* (`model.py:1338`), the minimal-agent mean-field step, the template responsibilities (`block_mlp.py:540`), the majorize-minimize step in `full_link_minimizer`, and the decoder KL; `test_free_energy.py`, `test_predictive_covariance.py` (38 tests) and the projected-gradient tests pass. Beyond the layer-1 finding, the headline cap fraction reports only the last layer (medium, `model.py:1888`). ### Transformer-ML

The evaluation protocol is sound: validation and test use sliding windows of 128 with stride 32 (`vfe3/data/datasets.py:757-803`), so every transition is scored exactly once with at least 96 tokens of context, and the baselines share `eval_stride` (`baseline.py:109`, `paper_arms.py:371`). The figure is GPT-2 BPE token-level perplexity, not comparable to word-level WikiText-103 numbers without conversion. Softmax axes, causal masking, the nine registered priors, comparison-space RoPE (position enters only through j−i) and the temperature match a conventional transformer.

At step 120k, 7 of 24 heads do no content routing (medium): layer 1's four uniform heads, and three heads that attend only to themselves (layer 2 heads 2 and 3, layer 3 head 0, entropy_norm between 1e-8 and 1e-10). With `attention_residual=False` the identity path is carried by the per-token frame and these self-attending heads (`gradients/relational.py:205-208`, `block.py:138-147`); a single-change residual arm would test that reading. The decoder is an uncontrolled confound against the baselines (high): the diagonal-KL decoder is linear in 2K features (`prior_bank.py:333-338`) where the baseline's linear decoder reads K (`baseline.py:633-640`), so the softmax-bottleneck rank bound is 2K+1 against K+1, and no `decode_mode="linear"` VFE arm exists. The "shared post-norm" baseline computes LN(h + a) (`baseline.py:196-198,753-756`) while the 32.65 base, having no attention residual, computes LN(a) (medium). The baseline learning rate of 3e-3 was selected on the 34.48 protocol (low here; the ml-engineer rates the same issue high, below), and one affine LayerNorm is shared by all six layers (`model.py:379-386`, `stack.py:129-130`; low).

### General sweep: core math and model

The pair KL can be computed as one centered GEMM that is about 3 times faster and about 100 times more accurate than the current TF32 tiled einsums (high; `relational_scoring.py:764-786`). On the trained checkpoint the current path's absolute error against fp64 is 0.29, 0.89 and 0.91 nats in layers 0, 2 and 3 (up to about 0.16 in a logit), while the expansion 2KL_ij = ⟨G_i + a_i a_iᵀ, P_j⟩ − 2a_i·(P_j b_j) + (b_jᵀP_j b_j + log det G_j) − r − log det G_i, with means centered by token 0's key mean and evaluated under `ieee_fp32_matmul`, gives 0.0021, 0.021 and 0.0073 nats, at 17 ms against 52 ms forward plus backward per layer at B=32. The exponential chart computes six fp64 φ1 matrix exponentials of size 256 per forward (`relational_operators.py:44,137`), which accounts for 36 of 52 forward host syncs; one batched `matrix_exp(O T) @ inv(T)` over the layer stack agrees to 2.2e-14 and reduces 72 syncs to 6 and 75 ms to 15 ms (medium). The `isclose` symmetry check (`relational_scoring.py:324,429`) runs on matrices already symmetrized bitwise and costs 17.3 ms per step (medium). The moment-matched covariance update spends about 40% of its CUDA time on strided permute copies of 268 MB tensors (`relational_operators.py:960-971`), which a (…, H, H, N, d²) layout would turn into one bmm (medium). The query-side Cholesky feeds only log det G_i, a row constant the softmax cancels (`relational_scoring.py:643,699`; low to medium), and the remaining per-forward host syncs (`relational_scoring.py:646`, `transport.py:1046,1103,1122`, `contracts.py:88`) leave the GPU idle about 35% of a step (low). The projection and spread tiling is necessary: without it a step used 29.7 GB and took about 40 s.

### General sweep: training, configuration and data

Rebuilding the test windows from the cache confirms the target accounting: 280,542 tokens, 8,764 windows, 280,541 scored targets (every transition), and 841,251 excluded positions (8,763 × 96 overlap context plus 3 padding).

Root cause: `vfe3/viz/report.py`'s single-run figure driver gated all three belief-UMAP figures' availability on `belief_controlled_ready`, which additionally required the loader to supply exactly the default 16,384-token "controlled" population (`CONTROLLED_BANK_TOKENS`, applied whenever `max_tokens`/`max_sequences` are both omitted -- the path `finalize_run` -> `_run_figures_isolated` -> `figure_worker.py` uses at the end of every training run). The exact-population requirement is not otherwise load-bearing at single-run time: `embedding_comparison.validate_comparison_records` already re-checks `sample.token_count` equality between sidecars at actual cross-run comparison time, so the report-time gate was redundant and happened to fail closed.

Reworded the now-inaccurate "skipped" warning to state that the figures still render at the short population and that the sidecar will not be comparable to a full 16,384-token run, and updated `generate_figures`'s docstring to match. ## Frame-parameter geometry: group_log retraction, belief-Fisher phi direction, content-link spectrum (2026-09-28)

This is a directly requested feature addition, not part of the `cleanup-consolidation-2026-09-22.md` punch list. The derivations, the verified implementation specs and the post-implementation amendments are in [derivations/2026-09-28-phi-natgrad-link-spectrum.md](derivations/2026-09-28-phi-natgrad-link-spectrum.md). The mathematics of the two optimizer routes is written up in [derivations/2026-09-28-phi-natgrad.tex](derivations/2026-09-28-phi-natgrad.tex) (24-page PDF alongside). Opus 5.5 wrote the derivations and the code; every derivation, each code track and the LaTeX note were adversarially reviewed on Fable 5.1, and a fixer applied the confirmed defects. Every existing config behaves exactly as before, and no nn.Parameter, optimizer state, checkpoint name or forward-pass behavior was added or changed.

**The exact group retraction.** The existing Frobenius-pullback phi step is projected Euclidean descent on the frame matrix $U=\exp\varphi$. For glk and untied block_glk its body direction has the closed form $\xi=U^{-1}\Pi_{\mathfrak g}(\bar G)$, where the ambient gradient $\bar G=L_{\log}(U^\top,M)$ is recovered exactly from the processed phi gradient by one Frechet derivative of the principal logarithm. The new field `m_phi_group_retraction` defaults to `"chart"`, which keeps the historical chart step and its backtracking; that route is bit-identical to before. Setting it to `"group_log"` retracts exactly, $\varphi\leftarrow\mathrm{Log}(e^{X}e^{-\eta s\xi})$, and halves only on a log, product-residual, descent or chart-bound failure.

A row uses the closed form only if it passes a Bendixson strip certificate and satisfies $\max_h\kappa_2(U_h)\le10^3$. Every other row uses the existing series direction, with that direction's conditioning certificate.

The new module `vfe3/geometry/matrix_log.py` holds the pure-torch float64 kernels: Denman-Beavers square roots, the principal logarithm, the log Frechet derivative by the pair method, and the strip certificate. The stager `stage_group_log_candidate` in `vfe3/gauge_optim.py` is generic over the direction it retracts. When group_log is active, metrics.csv gains six keys: `phi_group_fast_rows`, `phi_group_fallback_rows`, `phi_group_rechart_rows`, `phi_group_log_backtracks_max`, `phi_group_strip_margin_min` and `phi_group_fast_frame_cond_max`.

**The belief-Fisher natural gradient.** `m_phi_update_mode="fisher_group"` treats phi as the frame parameter of the reference belief $U^{-1}\cdot q$. It preconditions the M-step update with that belief's Fisher-Rao metric, pulled back to the frame and evaluated at the layer-0 prior-bank rows, which are the generative-model object. Phi remains a slow M-step variable, and deeper-layer posteriors are not used.

The metric is completed on its kernel, the stabilizer rotations, by the new field `m_phi_fisher_rotation_weight`. The default is 2.0, which makes the metric the Kronecker product $C^{-1}X(2C+\xi\xi^\top)$. The two row types are solved differently:

- Type rows are solved in closed form in $O(d^2)$ and count-normalized by $N_{\rm scored}/n_v$.
- Positional rows solve a pooled $d^2\times d^2$ operator with a Jacobi-scaled float64 Cholesky.

The route is stateless, applies no weight decay to phi, and takes its statistics from `PhiFisherStatisticsProvider` in `vfe3/train.py`. Rows off the principal strip are rejected with a zero step. It composes with either retraction; `group_log` is recommended because the chart route's $10^{-6}$ certificate caps steps near $7\times10^{-3}$ per block at trained norms. Validation admits glk or untied block_glk, per_token encoding, and the families gaussian_diagonal, gaussian_full and gaussian_diagonal_exact. When the route is active, metrics.csv gains its `phi_fisher_*` keys.

`m_phi_lr` and `m_pos_phi_lr` will need retuning for either manifold route. Neither route has been trained.

**The content-link spectrum.** `vfe3/metrics.py` computes the similarity invariants of the content links $L^{(h)}_{ij}=U_iM_hU_j^{-1}$. It reads them from the $d\times d$ matrix $W_h=\hat T_h\hat O_h$, which is invariant under the left gauge action, the right reference change and the per-head value basis. `_relational_diagnostics` emits ten `relational_link_*` keys per layer, with `val_` copies:

- spectral radius, minimum modulus and mean log-modulus;
- unit distance $\max|\lambda-1|$ and idempotency defect $\max|\lambda(\lambda-1)|$;
- the traces $\sum\mathrm{Tr}M_h$, $\sum\mathrm{Tr}M_h^2$ and $\sum_{h<g}\mathrm{Tr}M_hM_g$;
- the maximum and mean self-loop spectral radius. Under a causal prior the maximum equals the spectral radius of the whole-sequence fixed-routing map.

The readout dashboard and the posthoc report gained link-spectrum panels, columns and an eigenvalue CSV. The Omega-based `holonomy_deviation` and `holonomy_wilson` are now documented as coboundary closure monitors, not curvature; their CSV key names are unchanged.

**Launcher toggles.** `train_vfe3.py` and `ablation.py` now carry `m_phi_group_retraction="chart"` and `m_phi_fisher_rotation_weight=2.0`, and their `m_phi_update_mode` comment lists `"fisher_group"`. All existing values are unchanged.

October 1, 2026: removed the `compile_pair_kernel` toggle. The user reported no noticeable difference with it on and off, and asked for an investigation and a recommendation. Three findings made removal the right call. It was inert on the default route: `attention_mode="projected_predictive"` is the default in `config.py`, `train_vfe3.py`, `scaling.py`, and `ablation.py`, and that route never calls `belief_gradients`, so the knob's only consumer was unreachable; `config.py` already reported it in `inactive_fields`. Even when live on `attention_mode="transported"`, the compiled region was the closed-form pair kernel alone — elementwise arithmetic and two einsums — while `matrix_exp`, `eigh`, transport, the energy grid, and the softmax were deliberately outside it. And the backend does not engage on this machine: inductor codegen raises `RuntimeError: Compiler: cl is not found`, `torch.compile` defers that to the first invocation, and the call-site fallback then cached the eager kernel. Measured through the real `belief_gradients` path at N=16, K=8 on CPU with one thread, the first call cost 3234 ms against 1.9 ms eager, steady state was 0.7344 ms against 0.7354 ms over 200 repetitions, and the two outputs agreed exactly.

# 2026-10-08

## Audit 2026-10-08: critical and high remediation

This batch addresses the critical item and the high items of `docs/audits/audit-2026-10-08.md`. Architectural changes are opt-in toggles whose defaults reproduce the previous behavior. Numerical and bookkeeping corrections are applied directly.

Comparison-space RoPE (P1-1, P1-2). `relational_score` now rotates the projected moments under `ieee_fp32_matmul()`, the same pin as the moments it rotates, so under `fp32_matmul_precision="high"` the congruence `R_i G R_i^T` is no longer formed at TF32. The rotated route also keeps its source tuples. Rotating `a -> R_i a` and `G -> R_i G R_i^T` is exactly the projection through the per-token comparison map `R_i S`, so the tuples now carry `R_i S` (shape `(N, H, r, K)`). The source-root recovery, the QR and CholeskyQR3 rebuilds and the `cholesky_qr2` factor route then run on this route as they do without RoPE. Before the change, a failed float32 factor on this route raised `projected covariance is not positive definite`, and the code comment promised a float64 refactor that did not exist. `_projected_root_factor` tiles a per-token map along with the tokens.

Evaluation forwards (P0-1). `_eval_forward_with_precision_retry` replays a validation forward that raises at a reduced fp32 matmul policy one rung stricter, ending at `"highest"`, under `cfg.matmul_precision_retry`. It is used by `evaluate` (validation, the test pass and the train sample) and by `_eval_protocol_train_ce`.

Run artifacts (P1-6). A `PermissionError` from a Windows reader lock on `metrics.csv` no longer stops training. The row stays in memory and reaches the file at the next publish, which rewrites the whole history. `maybe_save_best` advances `best_val_ppl` and `best_step` only after `best_model.pt` is published, so a blocked save leaves the record consistent and the next better evaluation retries it.

Post-hoc M4 (P1-3). `vfe3/posthoc/routing.not_applicable_reason` refuses `rope_space="comparison"`, since the decomposition rebuilds the unrotated cost.

Router clamp (P1-4, toggle). The new field is `routing_kl_max_reference`, either `"absolute"` (the default and the previous behavior) or `"row_min"`. Under `"row_min"` the projected kernel router clamps `D_ij - min_{j admissible} D_ij`, where the shift is detached and admissibility follows the finite causal prior. Since the softmax is row-shift invariant, `beta` equals the exact softmax wherever no gap reaches `kl_max`. A row whose every cost exceeds `kl_max` keeps its cost order instead of routing uniformly with zero gradient. The live K=128 run (`20261007-210200`) has layer 1 fully capped in all four heads from step 40000 (`diagnostics/cap_activation.csv`). Under `"row_min"` the captured `score_energy_pre` and the cap counters see the shifted cost. The field is marked inactive off the projected kernel router.

Post-MLP norm (P1-5, toggle). The new field is `block_mlp_post_norm` (default `False`). It reapplies the block norm to the post-MLP mean, so the next router reads a normalized mean. The setting requires `block_coordinates="local"`, a block norm and a block MLP. The audit attributes the layer-1 saturation to the layer-0 MLP's token-common offset; that measurement was not repeated here.

Paper arms (P1-8). `ARMS` gains four manuscript M2 rows as single-change arms: "Frames frozen at their random initialization" (`gauge_transport="frozen"`, the pre-registered frame falsifier), "Affine content chart", "First-order content chart" and "Covariance passthrough instead of moment matching". The same four rows are added to `paper_artifacts.CONFIG["arms"]` with empty `runs`, since `tests/test_paper_arms_20260928.py` requires the two lists to agree. The row "Exponential chart with the channel receiver" still has no arm. `TIER_A` and `TIER_B` are unchanged.

Not applied (P1-7). The audit asks for an ALiBi fallback on every matched baseline and for the VFE ALiBi arm to drop the learned positional frames. `TIER_A` deliberately pairs the positionless "Per-type table of the frame table's size" with "Per-type read table, ALiBi (as run)", and a global fallback would make the two identical. The confound stands as reported and is left for a design decision.

Tests: `tests/test_audit_20261008_fixes.py` (12, tiny CPU models). They check that the rotated source root reproduces the rotated Gram, and that a rank-ill float32 comparison-RoPE score recovers on both factor routes to 1e-3 of the float64 reference; the HEAD version of `relational.py` raises on the same input. Under the launcher's numerics (`projected_cov_recovery_precision="fp32"`, `projected_cov_condition_recovery=False`) the score is finite and recovered but carries a maximum relative error of 0.11 against float64 on this rank-ill fixture, against 0.10 on the unrotated route: without the certificate only outright failures are rebuilt, on either route. They also cover the eval-forward ladder, a blocked metrics publish, the saturated-row behavior of both clamp references, the post-MLP norm toggle and its validation, the M4 refusal and the new arms.

The CPU fast lane (`run_cpu_tests.py`) ran 3858 tests with 9 failures. Four of them came from this batch (two `tests/test_train.py` fakes without `cfg`, and the two paper-arms tests that pin the row list and the tiny base's affine chart); they are fixed and pass on a focused rerun. The other five also fail on a clean HEAD worktree: the four so_n `gradient_*` goldens in `tests/test_son_phase0_20261005.py` (`relational_operators.O` gradient not bitwise equal) and `tests/test_semantics_multiseed.py::test_aggregate_seeds_end_to_end` (`KeyError: 'view/decode_W_causal/google_3cosadd'`). The runner stops after the first failing lane, so the slow lane did not run.

## Audit 2026-10-08: medium and low remediation

This batch addresses the medium and low items of `docs/audits/audit-2026-10-08.md`. Six implementers each worked on a separate group of files. No launcher configuration value changed and no config field was added. Each item was checked against the source before it was changed.

Model and numerics core. The projected diagnostics now take routing support from the finite causal prior, not from `beta > 0` (P2-19). The scalar `relational_score_klmax_frac` in `metrics.csv` still reports the last layer only, so layer saturation shows in the per-layer diagnostics. The kernel route's moment-matched covariance accumulates each row's spread about its own routed mean, in checkpointed query tiles. This equals the old uncentered moment in exact arithmetic and is far more accurate in float32 at large offsets (P2-20). The full-link receiver and the canonical-frame block MLP conjugation run under the IEEE-fp32 pin (P2-21). Full-link float64 retries now have their own counter, `receiver_law_retry_counter` (capture key `relational_receiver_law_retry_count`), instead of adding to the projected-covariance recovery count. Under fp64 recovery both KL factors are float64 even when only the query side was recovered. `mean_distance` routing is tiled and bit-identical to the direct grid. The comparison-space rotation is cached per (N, r, base, fraction, device, dtype), except under `inference_mode`. Routing dispatch goes through `ROUTING_COSTS` in both `relational.py` and `config.py`. On the mean-field minimal agent, a rotated query covariance that is not positive definite is flagged instead of raising. Under a state-dependent alpha the part deltas now share one frozen context. They therefore split the majorizer's delta, which upper-bounds the profiled cost delta. Two items are documented rather than changed: `decode_logits_amp` logits carry bf16 precision, and a damped diagonal receiver step (rho < 1) is not a descent step. The stale text about a two-hop block, a fifth row and a detach is removed from `free_energy.py`, `metrics.py` and `stack.py`. The RoPE banner is limited to frame-space RoPE.

Geometry. The Loewner divided differences are overflow-safe in float32. exp_bounded factors out exp(max), and log_floor uses the log1p form only for nearby arguments. `public_spd_repair_elements` counts floor repairs only, and ceiling contacts get a separate counter in the summary (P2-25). The clearance the repair lifts to is not specified anywhere in the code or docs, so it is unchanged. On the Fisher chart path, a frozen off-strip frame row is kept and counted in `rejected_rows` instead of raising (P2-24). Generator-free (`l0`) blocks are skipped in the per-block pullback direction, which is exact (P2-23). The off-strip Psi inverse gate now enforces the module's 1e-10 spectral budget. Manifold phi staging reads the generator shape without building the dense basis (P2-22, part). The dense positional `fisher_group` operator is unchanged: no exact structured solve was found, and a row-chunked or matrix-free alternative is a design decision. `conserved_S_drift` is NaN under comparison-space RoPE, because the rotation breaks the comparison-pair symmetry. Stale docstrings in `rope.py`, `transport.py`, `groups.py` and `lie_ops.py` are corrected. `content_link_edges="pure_gauge"` is now described as a coboundary control whose triangle holonomy is conjugate to the base link, not as flat (`config.py`, `edge_links.py`, `README.md`). When content links are on, the semantics frame report no longer calls the flat frame transport the only connection.

## Audit 2026-10-08: follow-ups to the items left open

This entry covers four items that the medium and low remediation left open, all decided by the user.

Positional Fisher operators (P2-22). `PhiFisherStatisticsProvider` no longer builds the full `(R, H, d^2, d^2)` float64 stack of pooled positional operators. It returns the middle-fiber moments of the distinct column types together with each column's gather index. `belief_fisher_group_direction` then forms and solves the operators a row chunk at a time through `pooled_operator_chunks`, under `POOLED_OPERATOR_CHUNK_BYTES` (256 MiB). Every row's operator depends only on its own batch column, so the chunked step is bitwise the unchunked one. At R = 128, H = 4, d = 32 the old stack was 4 GiB per table. Peak memory is now one chunk.

SPD repair clearance (P2-25). `_certify_public_spd` clips a failing row's spectrum to [eps + c, sigma_max - c] and rebuilds it in the public dtype. The clearance c = 8 K eps_dtype max(1, max|Sigma_ij|) is a worst-case roundoff bound and lifted lambda_min far above eps. Each failing row now takes the smallest clearance of the ladder c 2^-10, c 2^-6, c 2^-3, c that passes the same float64 Cholesky certificates. The last rung is the previous clearance, so every row the old repair certified is still certified, and the isotropic fallback is unchanged. On a float32 K = 32 row with entries near sigma_max = 100 and one eigenvalue below eps = 1e-6, the repaired lambda_min falls from 1747 eps to 1.6 eps.

Pullback structure-constant budget (geometer#2). The strict pullback M-step refused any basis wider than K = 12 (`max_k`). It now refuses a basis whose dense structure-constant workspaces, 3 · 8 n_gen^2 K^2 bytes, exceed `STRICT_STRUCTURE_BYTES` (512 MiB). With the new budget:

- `block_glk` per head is admitted up to d_head = 16; d_head = 17 needs 552 MiB and is refused.
- A shared-tied group is refused at full width, for example 256 generators on a 32 x 32 block.
- `_phi_group_rows_per_chunk` also counts the per-row (n_gen, K, K) complex matrix stacks.

`VFE3Config._pullback_structure_problem` applies the same budget at construction for every group with a pullback capability. A group whose generators act on one block is checked per block, using (n_gen_b, d_b). A tied group (`gauge_tying="shared"`, which includes so_n by default) is checked as one (n_gen, K) basis. This replaces the old `block_glk` `d_head <= 12` rule. `tests/test_frame_diagnostics_20261006.py` now uses d_head = 17 for its refusal case.

Scaling sizes (gap-stats). The offset law E + A N^-alpha has three parameters. `scaling_min_testable_sizes(with_offset)` therefore requires five distinct sizes for it and keeps four for the plain power law, and `scaling_analysis.py` reads the threshold for the configured fit form. The `scaling.py` width sweep gains a fifth width, `[20, 40, 80, 120, 160]`. The new 120 has d_head = 24 at `n_heads = 5`, which keeps the head width even for RoPE, and fills the largest gap on the log axis. A width of 320 would put about 1B parameters in the per-type frame table alone.

Tests: `tests/test_audit_20261008_followups.py` (5 tiny CPU tests). They check that a Fisher step with one-row chunks equals the unchunked step bitwise and that the step moves the positional rows. They also check the chunked operators against the dense operator, the repaired lambda_min below 10 eps with both certificates holding, the d_head 16/17 boundary and a shared-tying refusal at config time and at runtime, and the five-size threshold together with the five-width sweep.

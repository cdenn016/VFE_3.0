# October 7, 2026 changes

## Prototype template codebook figure

`template_codebook.png` printed raw token ids, and its per-layer text columns overran each other. The ids came from `feature_figures._token_label`, which called the dataset decoder as `decode(int(token))`; `get_tiktoken_decoder` returns a decoder over a list of ids, so every call raised, was swallowed, and fell back to the id. The private helper is removed, and `plot_template_codebook` and `plot_template_umap` use the shared `vfe3.viz.text.token_label`, which calls `decode([id])` and handles whitespace and invalid fragments. The codebook is now one panel per layer in two columns: a horizontal bar of each of the `top` (default 12) most-used templates' mean weight in percent, labeled by template index on the left, and on the right its `n_tokens` (default 4) most frequently won tokens, decoded and quoted, or "(none)" when it wins no report token. The figure of run `144.65_wikitext-103_K20_exact-proto-exact-posterior-LN` was redrawn from its saved `feature_probes.json`. Test: `test_template_codebook_decodes_tokens_with_the_sequence_decoder` in `tests/test_feature_diagnostics_20261006.py`.

## Launcher seeds and config dictionaries

The model seed was set twice in each launcher: `train_vfe3.py` carried `seed = 6` in `config` beside `NUM_RUNS`/`SEEDS` (a one-run launch had to keep `SEEDS[0]` equal to it), and `ablation.py` carried `seed = 6` in `BASELINE_CONFIG`, which every cell overwrote with `CONFIG["seed"]` or the sweep seeds. Both dictionary entries are removed. In `train_vfe3.py` the seed now comes only from `SEEDS`: `_resolve_seeds` returns `SEEDS[:NUM_RUNS]`, needs at least `NUM_RUNS` unique non-negative seeds, and refuses a `seed` key in the config dictionary. `ablation.py` keeps `CONFIG["seed"]` and the sweep `seeds` as before. The author's reformatted config dictionaries of both launchers and the working-tree `vfe3/config.py` (its default changes, e.g. `sigma_init=15`, `transport_chart_max_norm=50`, `predictive_covariance="moment_matched"`, and whitespace) are committed with this change, so the committed launchers resolve exactly as in the author's checkout. The key `e_q_phi_lr`, which is not a `VFE3Config` field and made both launchers fail to build, was removed at the author's decision. `tests/test_remediation_20260923_A.py` covers the seed resolution; the full suite was stopped at the author's request after 367 tests with no failure.

## Retirement of `phi_precond_mode` and the unused frame preconditioners

`phi_precond_mode` is removed from `VFE3Config`, at the author's request. No E-step reads it: the E-step frame update was already retired with `e_phi_lr`, and nothing in `vfe3/` called `precondition_phi_gradient`. The `pullback_group` M-step was its only reader, and that route required the value to equal the group's registered `pullback_capability`, which is `"pullback_per_block"` for `block_glk` and unset for every other group. So `none`, `clip`, `killing`, `killing_per_block` and `pullback` were either ignored or refused. On the shipped `block_glk` basis, which is Frobenius-orthonormal, the Killing modes were also only rescalings. The inverse metric is exactly (1/2K) I for `killing` (the center eigenvalue, lifted by the default `center_reg = 2K`, takes the same value) and (1/2d_h) I per block for `killing_per_block`. `clip` was a state-dependent scalar. Full `pullback` gives the same metric as `pullback_per_block` on `block_glk`, because for block-diagonal phi, d exp_phi(T_a) stays in the block of T_a, so every cross-block Frobenius entry is zero. A float64 CPU comparison at K=6 with H=1, 2, 3 agreed to 1.8e-15 or better.

`pullback_group` now dispatches the direction registered under `group.pullback_capability` (`stage_pullback_group_candidate`, `pullback_group_log_direction`, and `GaugeManifoldAdamW`, which lost the `phi_precond_mode` argument). The config check now refuses a group that has no capability. The `untied block_glk` requirement still refuses shared tying, which the two per-block-metric tying checks used to cover. In `vfe3/geometry/phi_preconditioner.py` the `_PRECOND` registry, `register_precond`, `get_precond`, `precondition_phi_gradient`, the six `_precond_*` rules, the Killing metric, its inverse builders and cache, `pullback_metric`, `pullback_metric_per_block`, the non-strict `_structure_constants` and the unused `_bch_chart_direction` are deleted. The module docstring now describes the per-block pullback direction it actually provides. Checkpoint parameter names are unchanged. Serialized configs that carry `phi_precond_mode` are not migrated, by the author's decision.

Launchers: the field is removed from `train_vfe3.py`, `ablation.py`, `scaling.py` and `parameter_optimization.py`. The author's working-tree edits to `train_vfe3.py`, `ablation.py` and `vfe3/config.py` are committed with this change. Tests: the dead-mode tests in `test_phi_preconditioner.py` (the legacy-golden, `none`, `clip`, Killing and ambient-pullback tests), the three `_bch_chart_direction` tests and the preconditioner-label test in `test_remediation_20260923_C.py`, the stale E-step `killing_per_block` forward test in `test_model.py`, and the Killing and per-block-metric tying tests in `test_gauge_tying_20261005.py` are removed. Tests that only passed `phi_precond_mode="pullback_per_block"` lost the argument. The registered-extension staging test now sets the group's `pullback_capability`. The shared-tying `pullback_group` refusal is asserted against the untied-`block_glk` message. `test_block_glmc.py` now matches the new no-capability message. Checks: the 18 changed test files plus `test_phi_group_spectral_differentials_20260808.py`, `test_config_retired_fields_20261002.py` and `test_lie_ops.py` (CPU, one thread) gave 442 passed and 1 failed. ## Confound controls for the per-type read-table comparison

The GL(K) v2 manuscript compares the evaluated K=128 realization (34.48 test perplexity) with a conventional transformer whose per-type read table nearly matches the frame table (55.10, `vfe3_runs/baselines/55.10_*`). That comparison leaves seven differences unisolated: where the per-type table acts (the frames read and write, the baseline table only reads), the optimizer, the positional mechanism (uniform causal prior with learned positional frames against ALiBi), the normalization layout (one shared post-norm against per-sublayer pre-norm), compute (the baseline trains about eleven times faster), the attention kernel (covariance-aware Gaussian KL against dot product), and seeds. This change adds the code for the controls; no run was launched.

`relational_routing="mean_distance"` (`vfe3/relational_scoring.projected_mean_distance_cost`) routes by half the squared distance of the projected means, the Gaussian KL with both projected covariances replaced by the identity, so it is the covariance-blind control for the routing kernel. It is nonnegative, so the cost bound applies unchanged, and like `noisy_query` it requires `attention_mode="projected_predictive"`. `vfe3/relational_scoring.routing_cost` maps routing names to cost functions for the posthoc interventions; `vfe3/gradients/relational.py` keeps its module-level dispatch.

`vfe3/baseline.py` gains `per_type_table="block_rw"`, which uses the frame-table-sized `(V, H, d, d)` table on both sides of attention as the realization uses its frames: the attention input is read through `exp(-X)` and the attention output written through `exp(X)`, block-diagonal on heads, identity at zero initialization. It also gains `norm_placement="post_shared"`, the realization's layout: one LayerNorm shared by every layer after the attention residual, the MLP residual on the normalized state, and no final normalization, which makes the context-processing parameter count exactly the realization's.

`paper_arms.py` now defaults to the 34.48 base (`k128_exp`), the new `base_protocol` budget (the base's own 150,000 updates and validation cadence), seeds `(6, 3), (23, 23), (54, 54)` and a baseline learning rate of 3e-3, the validation choice of the October 6 sweep; the 39.77 and 52.91 bases are kept and labeled as the earlier study. A baseline arm may declare `overrides`, and a protocol field among them is a declared difference. New arms, also listed in `paper_artifacts.py`: "Per-type read table, ALiBi (as run)" (the 55.10 configuration), its single-change variants with the realization's optimizer (Adam beta_2 0.999, no matrix decay, decay 0.1 on the table), with a read-and-write table, and with the shared post-norm, plus the VFE arms "Learned frames, ALiBi prior" and "Covariance-blind routing (mean distance)". With a causal-prior base, the existing "Per-type table of the frame table's size" row is the read table under the realization's prior. `TIER_A` (baseline controls and the compute-matched transformer) and `TIER_B` (evaluated replicates, identity frames, identity frames with an equal additive table, ALiBi realization, mean-distance routing) name the two launch sets; `CONFIG["arms"]` accepts either name. A dry run on the 34.48 base resolves every arm with only its declared changes; Under `base_protocol` the evaluated arm's replicate at the base run's own seed pair, (6, 3) from `provenance.json`, is the 34.48 run itself: `paper_arms.completed_run` returns it, so it is skipped in training, listed in the manifest, and read by the compute-matched rule. TIER_B then estimates 229 GPU-hours. The identity-frame arms also remove the positional frames, a coercion of `gauge_transport="off"`, so they change positions as well as frames.

Tests: `tests/test_confound_controls_20261007.py` (9 tests: the mean-distance cost against the identity-covariance KL and its independence of covariances, dispatch, a tiny-model forward and backward, `block_rw` identity at zero initialization, its inverse read and write and gradient, the post-norm count and gradient, the resolution of both tiers, and the reuse of the base run as the evaluated replicate). `tests/test_paper_arms_20260928.py` accounts for the new group and for the ALiBi arm being inapplicable on an ALiBi base, and its dry-run test selects every arm explicitly. The baseline, paper-arms, noisy-query, posthoc routing and interventions, relational scoring, minimal-agent, precision, condition-recovery, frame-escalation and semantics suites pass on CPU with one thread.

# Problem contract and typed foundations

Freeze the target before opening the portfolio. State nonempty exact quantifiers, domains and codomains, regularity, measures, boundary conditions, symmetry actions, the equivalence relation, premises, modeling postulates, permitted theorems, desired output, falsification criterion, and literature policy. Distinguish definitions from assumptions, postulates, conjectures, search priors, and desired conclusions. Confirm that every domain is nonempty and every displayed map, action, quotient, section, pullback, functional, derivative, and integral is well-defined. Declare `negative_certificate_kind` as either `COUNTEREXAMPLE` or `NONEXISTENCE_PROOF`. A universal target uses a counterexample and an existential target uses a nonexistence proof. A mixed target must choose explicitly from the frozen quantifier structure; the validator does not infer one.

Record an affirmative request only as `SEARCH_PRIOR_AFFIRMATIVE` in `problem-contract.target.search_priors`. The search prior may affect effort allocation. It may not occur in premises, modeling postulates, declared assumptions, evidence, claims, dependency edges, certificates, adversarial conclusions, or reports. Do not silently strengthen premises to make the desired theorem true.

For probability claims, specify sample spaces, sigma-algebras, normalized laws, support, absolute continuity, domination, regular conditional laws or disintegrations, integrability, and every exchange of limits, derivatives, expectations, or integrals. For geometric claims, type the bundles, group actions, representations, sections, connections, quotients, and transition data. For physical claims, state dimensions, units, observables, and every operational or modeling bridge.

Serialize the complete `target` object as UTF-8 canonical JSON with sorted keys, no insignificant whitespace, and no non-finite numbers. Its full SHA-256 is `target_digest`, and `contract_id` is `contract-sha256-<target_digest>`. Put the schema, contract ID, and target digest in every JSON artifact and in the first-line JSON metadata header of every Markdown artifact. The target claim repeats the exact target statement, kind, and quantifiers and carries the same digest, which binds every remaining structured target field. Any change to the target, assumptions, equivalence, or literature policy creates a new contract revision and invalidates dependent certification.

The literature policy names allowed databases, dates, languages, primary-source requirements, and stopping rules. A bounded search is not a worldwide search and cannot establish a priority claim. Record unavailable sources and novelty limits as unresolved obligations rather than filling them from memory.
